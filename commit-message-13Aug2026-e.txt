The half-year labels are in the store, so test the fallback rather than buy the data

Measured, and it withdraws the reading rather than the finding.

oag_label_check --like 201%-H% returns six labels, each carrying exactly two regions:

  2015-H1  1,410,747 rows      2015-H2  1,522,075
  2016-H1  1,516,193           2016-H2  1,620,404
  2017-H1  1,647,641           2017-H2  1,735,757

Five monthly regions plus two half-year regions is seven. The world is complete for 2015 to
2017 and it is split across two label forms. So REGION-COVERAGE's reading that the store is
SHORT of intra-African and intra-Gulf flying is withdrawn, nothing needs buying, and nothing
needs downloading. Handover item 6 is not an Egnyte load.

THE FINDING SURVIVES THE CORRECTION. REGION-CONSEQUENCE said cohorts 2016 and 2017 measured
capa, qcx and legs_n against a world missing that connectivity, and that is still true,
because load_legs reads ONE label. On a monthly label it sees five regions. On a half-year
label it would build "2017-H1-15", which is not a date, and return nothing. The data is
present and unreachable from either side. The diagnosis changes and the remedy goes from an
OAG purchase to a query.

WHAT THIS COMMIT ADDS is the fallback tested before it is written into load_legs.
oag_label_check --month YYYY-MM --coverage IATA,IATA counts distinct departing flights at
the named airports in load_legs' own 15th-to-21st window on eff_from and eff_to, three
ways: the monthly label alone, the half-year label alone, and the two together. Distinct
counting is deliberate, so an overlap between labels is counted once.

The control is inside the test. A monthly-region airport must gain nothing while an African
or Gulf one is restored to a normal month. If both move, the labels overlap and the union is
not a clean reconstruction, and we know that before a cohort is rebuilt.
