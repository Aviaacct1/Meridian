Stop the cross-check: three basis errors in an hour, all mine, all the same family

--min-months DOES NOT SELECT YEAR-ROUND SERVICES. bt2_profile counts months_operated from
the launch month to year end, so 12 means the route launched in January, not that it runs
year-round. A July launch flying every month thereafter reads 6. So --min-months 11 selected
January and February launches, 33 long-haul routes, and discarded every year-round service
that started later in the year. The output said so and I did not read it: median 12, p10 11,
p90 12.

That is the third correction on this script inside an hour. First the seats were a partial
year against a full one. Then annualising them assumed every route becomes year-round. Now
the filter meant to separate those two cases turns out to select launch month instead. Each
correction revealed the next, which is the signal that the method is wrong rather than the
arithmetic.

THE LINE IS STOPPED. The join loses 1,479 of the arm's 3,072 rows before any filter and 33
survive after two. The script's own guard fired and the rule comparison from that run must
not be quoted. A fourth patch would be fitting the method to the data.

THE CLEAN VERSION, if the idea is worth testing at all, is an arm at --offset 0. It grades
against the launch year and puts seats, traffic and forecast on one set of months, with no
annualising and no seasonality question. Forty-five minutes, and it removes the whole class
of error rather than the instance.

WHAT SURVIVES TONIGHT is one figure, and it survives because it contains no seats and needed
no join correction. On 730 long-haul international FSC routes the engine's captured_uncapped
reads a median 0.617 of actual local outturn, landing within +-20% on 13.4% and within +-10%
on 5.9%. On the 33 January launches it reads 0.473, so the under-read may deepen on routes
with a full launch year, though 33 is not a sample.

The engine under-reads the long-haul local market by circa 1.6x. The clamp and the switch
cannot repair it: the synthetic check established they only reach routes wrong in the tail
and this is a fault in the middle. A direct level correction by haul and carrier type,
measured the way FLOOR-EVIDENCED did it for the connecting side, is the smaller and
better-posed job, and it needs no gauge and no join.
