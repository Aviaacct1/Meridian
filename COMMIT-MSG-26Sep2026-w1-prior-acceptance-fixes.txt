W1: schedule prior, three fixes from the first workstation acceptance (26 Sep night)

- Below-floor pairs not keyed on market size. SJC-TPE's own pair records 212 passengers a year
  (the market books through SFO), under the 250 floor of every launch in the record
  (bt2_forecast.TRAIN_MIN_BASE). It was classed S0 and bounded to 3x only (CI A359 3x, 83,538).
  The market band is now left unkeyed under the floor, the lookup falls to the haul-only row, and
  the payload says why in schedule_prior.pair_gaps.
- In-range gauge first. When the prior is loaded and any in-range row plans inside the load
  factor band, out-of-range ("nearest to band") rows are not eligible for the headline. Before
  this, CI's A359 at 306 seats (nearest) beat an in-range B789 at 278 on contribution alone.
- Unchecked runway said. When the chosen type's airfield band is UNKNOWN, airfield_note says the
  runway check has not been made, rather than staying blank (SOU-JFK, UA B753).

Acceptance: pull, restart, the same three-pair probe; SJC-TPE shows level 4 and a pair_gaps line;
SOU-JFK shows a non-blank airfield note if the B753 is UNKNOWN.
