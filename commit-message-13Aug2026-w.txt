A perfect level, fitted on the same routes it scores, reads BELOW the uncorrected figure.
Every segmented version lands inside a point of doing nothing. The reason is in the IQR: it
straddles 1.0 on every cut, so the engine over-reads on the lower quartile and under-reads
by six times or more on the upper one in the same population, and a median shift moves mass
out of the band on one side faster than it moves it in on the other.

THE FINDING UNDERNEATH IT is that both legs of the engine are now measured as route-level
uninformative. The IQR of actual over forecast spans a factor of 8.1 on all routes, 8.6
international and 4.2 on long-haul FSC. FEED-IS-UNINFORMATIVE said the same of the
connecting side in July at an IQR of 2.666 in logs. The total looks plausible because the two
errors partly cancel and the plan cap hides the rest.

THE ONE CUT THAT CHANGES THE SIGN is domestic against international. Domestic n=347 reads a
median 0.799 with 17.0% in band, so the engine over-reads there. International n=2,601 reads
2.066 with 10.6%. That is a factor of 2.6 between the medians and the largest conditional
difference measured today, and it still does not earn its place, because the spread inside
each is far larger than the gap between them. Carry it as a diagnostic, not a correction.

WHAT IT DOES TO THE WIRING, which is where the day opened. The thing the calibrated model
would replace is now measured at 11.3% within +-20% at route level and is not repairable by
a level on any segment tried. The pin score already had the calibrated model at 22.4%
against the Cortex path's 12.6% on the same 1,555 routes, p<0.0001. So the case for wiring is
stronger tonight than it was this morning, and the blocker is unchanged and is John's: which
quantity the claim attaches to.
