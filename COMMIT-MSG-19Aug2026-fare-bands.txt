R5: measured fares leave self-serve surfaces as bands, never exact

The Work Order permits repackaged analysis and forbids extracts; a measured
market fare printed in currency to the cent is a Sabre statistic travelling
whole, and the tool's own written rule was index-only for fares. Landed ahead
of the Sabre demo of Thursday 20 August.

New:
- app/fare_bands.py   the one banding rule: fixed grid ($25 under 500, $50 to
  1,500, $100 above), stated rather than proportional so the exact figure is
  not recoverable from the band edges. Labels carry no currency symbol; each
  surface adds its own.
- app/test_fare_bands.py   22 checks: the grid, the deck helper agreeing with
  it at the grid boundaries, and every surface asserted banded with its exact
  form gone.

Changed:
- app/cortex_app.py   the forecast payload carries avg_fare_band and no exact
  avg_fare; the opportunities endpoint bands its per-market fare too (that
  endpoint is iterable across airports, and exact fares per market would be a
  fares table by another name).
- app/cortex_dashboard.html   the summary row renders the band.
- app/cortex_workbook.py   the assumptions fare line states the band, marked
  as one; the P&L inside the workbook still runs on the exact assumption
  server-side.
- deck/forecast_spec.py   _fare_band_label prefers the payload's band and
  bands a raw figure itself on older payloads; both deck assumption surfaces
  read it. Same grid as fare_bands.py as a literal (deck import path), with
  the change-both-or-neither warning in both files.

Unchanged, deliberately: exact fares inside Avia-delivered engagement work
(the deck_from_cases contract path), and every server-side computation.

Verified: test_fare_bands 22, test_attribution 28, test_visual_layer 32,
test_forecast_pack_maps 16, all 0 failed; dashboard script parses.
