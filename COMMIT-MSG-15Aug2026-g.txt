The vintage problem, and a correction to my own claim about the P2P wiring

WHY NOTHING MOVED. od_source_why on SJC-BOS: at 2025 every guard passes except the
year, and the store spans 2000-2024; at 2024 every guard passes. The cause is
structural rather than a fault. DOT publishes a year or more behind, the engine's base
year is 2025, and _year_complete refuses correctly. Left alone, DOT would only ever
answer a back-test and the US proposition would be undeliverable. This recurs every
year, so it needed answering rather than working around.

I WAS WRONG ABOUT THE POINT TO POINT LEG AND SAID SO ON JOHN'S SCREEN. I claimed the
live P2P leg had never been wired to od_source, from a grep of cortex_app without
following the field back. route_forecast line 488 DOES call od_source.market_split, and
line 511 handles the consequence properly: the GDS coverage gross-up is set to 1.0 when
DB1B answered, DB1B being a full-market actual rather than a sample. The three
destination_market_split calls in cortex_app compute catchment SHARES and growth rates,
not the P2P market level. Corrected in od_source_why.py, which was printing the wrong
claim.

app/cortex_app.py
  The payload's od_source.point_to_point now reads r["od_source"], which route_forecast
  already reports, rather than a feed_cfg key nothing sets. As written it would have
  printed Sabre on a DOT run.

app/od_source.py
  Vintage indexing, OFF unless AVIA_OD_INDEX_VINTAGE is set, because it is a method
  choice and not a fix. When the requested year is outside the store, DOT's most recent
  COMPLETE year is read and carried forward on Sabre's OWN growth for the same markets
  and the same quantity, so the LEVEL is DOT's and only the year-on-year movement is
  Sabre's. Aggregate rather than per market: a per-market factor on a thin feeder is
  mostly noise. A factor outside 0.5 to 2.0 is refused and the run falls back to Sabre,
  since a market does not halve or double in a year and a figure that says it did is two
  populations rather than one. The vintage and the factor go INTO the source label: a
  reader told DOT and not told the year would take a 2024 measurement for a 2025 one.

app/route_feed.py
  The two Sabre closures take an optional year, so od_source can ask for a second year
  when it indexes. Default is the run's year, unchanged.

bt2/od_source_feed_check.py
  Three cases added, 9 of 9 pass. Indexing off and a year outside the store falls back;
  indexing on returns DOT's level times Sabre's growth with the vintage and the factor
  in the label; an implausible growth factor falls back rather than publishes.
