Watch page visual layer (Job 1, 16 August) and the season/year label batch

The Watch page gets its two charts in the Observatory grammar, the page moves
off the old Georgia/#B8860B palette onto the shared tokens, and the dashboard
now states season and year beside every headline figure, which is the labelling
that sent three acceptance runs to seasonal totals read against annual pass
marks on 15 August.

- route_watch.daily_seats: departing seats by day of week, latest snapshot
  against the year-earlier one. DEPARTING, not two-way: a based rotation
  counts twice summed both ways (John's ruling, 15 August). Dedupe to one
  record per (carrier, flight_no, dep, arr, local_dep_time) with max(mask)
  BEFORE summing, the frequency_frame trap: the store repeats one schedule
  record per region label. A record with no operating-day mask is counted
  out and named, never spread.
- pick_weeks fix, found by the new fixture test: an airport holding a
  single weekly label fell through to an empty monthly spine and reported
  "no snapshots" while holding one. One label now answers, with the stated
  no-comparison note. capacity_moves behaviour otherwise unchanged.
- airport_profile.read_t100_monthly: read_t100's rules (seg BY NAME, class
  F only, origin = departing onboard) at monthly grain. read_aci_monthly:
  aci_monthly by year and month, two-way; an unfiled month is a gap, never
  a zero. Both refuse rather than probe.
- /api/watch/series: the two chart series plus store freshness. US airports
  read the DOT census, elsewhere ACI, no fallback across that boundary; the
  payload names the measured basis and the page derives the other, marked ~
  with the factor of two stated (the standing both-bases ruling). Freshness
  reads AVIA_REFRESH_STATUS (refresh_pickup's wrapper writes it), so
  September's unwatched refresh test is visible on the page a tester opens.
- cortex_watch.html: Observatory tokens and fonts (Newsreader/Inter/IBM
  Plex Mono, brass #D4A249); both plates in the dashboard's grammar:
  registration corner ticks, brass observed, ink dashed comparator, direct
  labels never legends, Newsreader italic annotation, provenance rail under
  every figure with source, units, period and method, actuals stated.
  Human dates via pretty_label on chart labels; ISO stays in the mono rail.
  Also fixed in passing: a competitor block carrying {error} rendered empty
  tables; the error now shows.
- Label batch (dashboard): the "Schedule" box is renamed Season with the
  season-totals warning in its title; the headline basis and the Total
  forecast card state forecast year and season span; the slider note states
  the span and year next to the figures. The Watch demand header already
  names its source (shipped 15 August); verified, not re-done.
- test_watch_series.py: 20 fixture checks, no stores needed: the region
  duplicate counted once, dotted masks, the maskless record named, the
  arrival-only and freighter exclusions, the T-100 twin-table refusal,
  charter exclusion, arrears months absent, the ACI gap-not-zero rule, and
  the single-snapshot answer. All pass, as do the existing fixture suites
  (test_airport_profile 36, test_refresh_pickup, test_feed_provenance).
