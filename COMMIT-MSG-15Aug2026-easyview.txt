Easy view and departure chart: John's 15 August corrections

The easy view gains the two inputs a client actually argues about and loses
the one that was an engine switch in client clothes:

- Seat count box beside the aircraft: blank sizes from the carrier's own
  configuration or the type table as before; after a run the placeholder
  shows the seat count used and its source, so overrides start from a
  stated number. Sent to /api/forecast now and to /api/optimise ready for
  when that endpoint takes seats.
- Stimulation posture in plain words: Standard (calibrated type factor),
  Conservative (1.00, no stimulation), Aggressive (type +0.20, a labelled
  working assumption). The Expert box still overrides with an exact value.
- Connectivity floor moved to Expert mode.

Expert mode split into Judgement and Calibration groups. Judgement (market,
share, stimulation, growth, year, attractiveness, radius, cabin, plan LF)
shows as before. Calibration constants (coverage, feed rates, dominance,
connection coefficients, circuity, indirect factor, the floor) sit behind
one confirming click that says the back-test set them. Everything stays
overridable per the house rule; nothing is merely hidden. Stale 0.85 cabin
placeholder now reads measured.

The departure chart now stands alone: forecast year, basis and unit on the
SVG itself; the point to point leg drawn as a shaded band between the total
and connecting series (one carried basis, so the gap IS the P2P leg, and on
a bound route the band visibly narrows as connecting crowds P2P out); the
caption names the band and states that scoring is on the outbound
departure.
