Each movement screened on its own time: the rotation is not a loop

JOHN'S RULING, 14 August: a curfew blocks every movement in its window, arrivals and
departures alike. And his correction before it: the aircraft does not shuttle. It flies
another route from the destination and comes back, so the return departure is a FREE
VARIABLE and its arrival at the origin is not dep + 2 x block + turn.

Four movements, and only two of them belong to the outbound decision.

app/route_feed.py
  optimise_departure now screens the outbound on ITS OWN two movements: the departure
  from the origin against the origin's hours, and the arrival at the hub against the
  hub's. The two derived tests are gone. They blocked departures that are perfectly
  legal, because a return time exists that lands outside the window whatever the outbound
  does: on SJC-TPE against a 23:00-06:00 origin curfew the derived return-arrival test
  alone blocked 17:30 through 04:00, most of the evening, for a rotation nobody flies.
  The permitted window is therefore WIDER than it was and the optimum may move.

app/cortex_app.py
  _schedule_times times the return on its own two movements. From the earliest possible
  turn it steps forward in quarter-hours across a whole day until BOTH the departure from
  the destination and the resulting arrival at the origin fall outside their own airport's
  restricted hours. Where no such time exists in 24 hours it draws the earliest turn and
  SAYS SO in inbound_need rather than drawing an illegal return as though it were a
  schedule. An unreadable window is reported the same way.
  The restricted hours are now resolved once and unconditionally: they were read inside
  the branch that only runs when an airline is named and no departure was given, while
  the schedule builder needs them on every path.

app/cortex_dashboard.html
  The schedule line carries "RETURN NOT TIMED" and the reason when the search failed.
  And the curfew line now reads "best permitted departure 06:30 ... would depart 00:30 if
  unrestricted", where it read "departs 06:30 within the restriction", which parses as the
  departure being INSIDE the curfew. On a page an airport takes to a curfew review that is
  the sentence they stop on.

Tested on SJC-TPE geometry, block 13h45: no curfew reproduces the previous return exactly
(08:15 arriving 06:00, unchanged); a 23:00-06:00 origin curfew returns 14:15 arriving
12:00, both legal; a window ending at 22:00 correctly permits a 22:00 arrival, windows
being half-open; an origin closed 00:00-23:59 finds nothing and says so; and a curfew
typed with "to" now parses.
