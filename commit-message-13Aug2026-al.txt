warm_demo signed in at /login and the app has served /signin since the entry rebuild

The portal launched and the warm-up failed with:

  FAILED: could not log in (HTTP Error 404: Not Found). Check AVIA_PASSWORD.

which pointed at the password, and the password was not the problem. warm_demo posts to
/login in two places, the readiness probe and the sign-in, and cortex_app has served /signin
since the entry page was rebuilt. Nothing had run this path since the rename, which is the
same shape as the --pairs report reading a variable that had moved: a tool nobody exercises
until the day it is needed.

Both are corrected to /signin, and the form now carries next and email as
cortex_app.signin_post parses them.

AND THE SIGN-IN NOW PROVES ITSELF. It checks that an obs_entered cookie came back rather
than treating a successful HTTP call as a successful sign-in. cortex_app returns a 303 to
/signin?err=1 when a password is refused, which is a perfectly good HTTP response, and that
would otherwise have surfaced much later as an unexplained redirect on the first warm
forecast.

Every other endpoint warm_demo touches was checked against cortex_app rather than only the
one that failed, because a single stale path implies the file has drifted: /signin,
/api/pitch/health, /, /trackrecord and /methodology all exist. Only /login was dead.

Worth recording that the engine pin worked on the same run: "forecast engine: QSI (the
shipped QSI engine) [shell said bt2, overridden]". The safeguard caught a live bt2 export in
the launching shell on its first outing.
