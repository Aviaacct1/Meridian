DOT DB1B: coupon-split store, the label rule, and two dead config paths

Job 1 groundwork. Nothing in the engine changes: AVIA_OD_SOURCE still defaults to
sabre and od_source is still off.

WHY THE COUPON SPLIT IS NEEDED. od_market carries one figure per origin, destination
and year over every itinerary type. That matches the point to point leg, which reads
all itineraries through sabre_catchment.destination_market_split. It does NOT match
the feed legs: route_feed.connecting_market line 203 and behind_market line 345 both
filter to connecting itineraries only. Dropping od_source into those legs as od_market
stands would swap a connecting-only market for a total market and inflate the feed base
by the whole nonstop share. The raw DB1BMarket extracts carry MktCoupons, which is the
itinerary length, and bt2/bt2_db1b.py already reads them that way.

app/build_db1b_coupons.py (new)
  Builds od_market_coupons(origin, dest, year, quarter, coupons, pax, avg_fare) from
  the raw extracts at E:\Avia\Usmarket data. Coupons 1 is nonstop, 2 is a single
  connection and matches the live feed filter exactly, 3 or more is collapsed to 4 and
  kept rather than dropped. Passengers grossed x10, the convention already recorded in
  bt2_db1b.py line 3. NO filters applied: no bulk fare exclusion, no country test, no
  fare range, because the convention behind od_market is not recorded anywhere in the
  repo and the first build has to reproduce it before anything is changed. Quarters are
  logged built, missing or empty; an extract that parses to nothing is logged empty
  rather than counted as built, which is the shape an older vintage fails in. Re-running
  skips quarters already built.

app/db1b_coupons_check.py (new)
  Four checks against od_market: TOTAL, PAIR on three dense and three thin pairs,
  SPLIT SANE, and QUARTERS. Tested in both directions on synthetic extracts: the
  correct build passes 13 of 13; an od_market built nonstop-only fails TOTAL at +302%
  and every PAIR; a year missing one quarter fails QUARTERS and TOTAL at -25.7%.

app/od_source.py
  Year-span test. The DOT label was kept whenever DB1B returned nothing, on the
  reasoning that DB1B could see the market and did not, which is the EAS and commuter
  tail. That reasoning does not hold for a base year outside the store's own span:
  there DB1B reads nothing for EVERY US market, the engine reads Sabre for all of them,
  and the label must say Sabre. The store spans 2015-2024, so a 2025 base year would
  have put "US DOT O&D Survey (DB1B)" on a slide produced entirely from a Sabre run.
  Docstring records that DB1B is US domestic only, measured on the store (no rows for
  TPE, LHR, NRT, CDG or YYZ), and that SJC-TPE therefore has no all-US market on any
  leg, since route_feed measures the behind leg from each feeder to the route
  DESTINATION rather than to the origin.

bt2/od_source_label_check.py (new)
  Four label cases on a synthetic store, no real data needed. 4 of 4 pass, and verified
  it bites: with the year test removed a 2025 run returns Sabre's number under the DOT
  label.

app/config.py
  _env_store resolver, taking the first candidate that exists. T100_DUCKDB and
  FORM41_DUCKDB both pointed at the E:\Avia root while both stores live under
  E:\Avia\Usmarket data, so neither resolved and every reader took a silent blank.
  DB1B_COUPONS_DUCKDB added.
