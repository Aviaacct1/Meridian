Contract forecast table: one basis, every column from its own payload key

The 18 August defect, fixed at its home: forecast_to_contract's
_fill_forecast_table patched gaps by dividing CAPTURED-after-stimulation by
the stimulation factor and comparing it against the TWO-WAY pair market, so
the pack's Traffic forecast slide printed -80.6% growth and a row that did
not multiply through. Found the night the three-airline tables were built
from the contracts.

The row is now SET, not patched, on one stated each-way basis, the same
decomposition the workbook uses: base decomposed from the grown market at
the payload's own growth rate (schedule.growth_rate/growth_years, added
18 Aug); the service-year column is the grown market; stimulation stated;
capture is the EFFECTIVE rate after the capacity allocation so
grown x stim x capture = forecast in the reader's own arithmetic; forecast
is the carried figure. Connecting legs decompose the same way (their bases
arrive grown), state their 1.0 stimulation, and a leg with no base keeps
its named gap, never a zero. A _basis line on the block states all of it.
