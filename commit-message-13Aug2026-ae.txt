Found what built the published pair: bt2_mixed_basis, guarded and its output fixed

bt2/bt2_mixed_basis.py is what produced the mixed basis, and it was in the repo all along
with an importable attach(). It grades US domestic launches against DOT DB1B and everything
else against Sabre MIDT, which is John's rule of 5 August, and the db1b_outturn_2016 to 2024
files it needs are already in bt2_relaxed.

Its own docstring states the coverage honestly: DB1B Market quarters run 2000 to 2024 with
2016 Q1 absent and nothing for 2025, so the 2016 cohort is scaled from three quarters and
flagged, and US domestic launches in 2025 keep their Sabre grading. Lines 151 to 155 print
Sabre throughout against mixed, blind and calibrated, which is exactly the comparison a site
restatement needs. So the published pair is reproducible after all.

GUARDED, because AVIA_BT2_TARGET could not collide with this before today. attach() overwrites
r["actual"] with the DOT figure on US domestic launches, and DB1B is a local nonstop ticket
count. Dropping it on top of the sector target would grade 595 routes on the local market and
5,929 on the whole sector inside one sample, which is not a mixed ruler but a broken one. It
now refuses by name unless the target is nonstop.

The build stamp is printed here too, imported from bt2_claimset rather than copied, so the
two lines cannot drift.

AND THE SAME LITERAL-PERCENT FAULT I MADE IN oag_label_check THIS MORNING, found in this file
while adding the guard. Line 155 passed "  calibrated within +-10%%" as a label argument,
where the doubled percent is not consumed by the format operator and printed as "+-10%%", and
it put 0.0 in the blind column as a placeholder that reads as a measurement of zero. It now
prints both tolerances for both bases, four lines, no placeholders.
