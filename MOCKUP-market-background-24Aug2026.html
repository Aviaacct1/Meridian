Design intent
The strip renders automatically once origin, destination and airline resolve, before anything is run; the body opens on click and the open/closed choice is remembered per user. Everything above is measured market fact from stores already loaded (Sabre, OAG, GeoNames), assembled by one new endpoint; no forecast content appears here, so the panel can never disagree with a run. Charts reuse the dashboard's existing bar/provenance patterns; the catchment cell reuses the live Leaflet map. Each cell downloads as SVG/CSV. The brief never blocks the Run button: it fills in while assumptions are set, cached per route and week.
