# Routes World 2026 (Frankfurt, 21-23 October): who is registered, read 21 September 2026

Source: the Routes World 2026 matchmaking platform (Grip), read from John's signed-in account on
21 September 2026, 1,853 registered delegates. Organisations only, with delegate headcount in
brackets; no delegate names are recorded here by design. Registrations grow until the show:
re-read in the week of 12 October and add to the pre-warm set. Avia stand: F174.

## 1. The shape of the show

706 organisations. Airports 353 organisations, 907 delegates (49% of everyone). Airlines 187
organisations, 600 delegates. Suppliers, consultancies and media 91 organisations, 201
delegates. Tourism authorities and destinations 75 organisations, 145 delegates.

Airports by country (organisations): United States 39, United Kingdom 22, Germany 21, France
21, Italy 19, Canada 16, China 15, Japan 10, Australia 8, India 8, Poland 8, Zimbabwe 8, Mexico
6, Ireland 6, Viet Nam 6, Saudi Arabia 5, UAE 5, Brazil 5, Philippines 5, Austria 5, Netherlands
5, Spain 4, Croatia 4, Denmark 4, then a long tail of one to three each.

What that says for the stand: Europe is roughly half the airports in the room and the US a
further tenth, so the pre-warm set and the rehearsed demo panel must be European-heavy with a
transatlantic spread, not built around long-haul Asia. The buyer we planned for (a route
development team pitching routes) is the largest single population at the show.
