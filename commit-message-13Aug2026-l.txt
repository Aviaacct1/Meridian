The grading year does not explain the feed, and a status line of mine that lied

GRADING-YEAR-AS-THE-RULER IS REFUTED AS A MATERIAL EXPLANATION, and it was my hypothesis.

Connecting traffic on the same route, later year over earlier:

  Y2 over Y1   median 1.039   n=720     P2P on the same routes 1.013   n=1,287
  Y3 over Y1   median 1.136   n=328     P2P 1.104                      n=550
  Y3 over Y2   median 1.045   n=336

The differential is the point. Connecting-specific growth over the local leg is x1.026 at
Y2 and x1.029 at Y3, so 3% across two years, which is market growth rather than maturation.
Closing RECUT-RESULT's tenfold over-read on maturation alone would have needed a Y2 over Y1
of 10.2. The ruler is sound and RECUT-RESULT stands as measured: the shipped feed level
over-reads by circa ten times and the grading year does not explain it.

THE SECOND READING IS WORTH MORE THAN THE FIRST. The share of routes rising at all is 53.5%
at Y2 and 52.2% on P2P, a coin flip, and the IQR of connecting Y2 over Y1 runs 0.742 to
1.356, a spread of 1.83 across the middle half. Cohort 2016 reads 1.075 and cohort 2017
reads 0.995 on the same measure, so the direction is not consistent between two adjacent
cohorts. Route-level connecting traffic moves circa 30% either way year to year, so the
quantity the feed is graded against is itself volatile. That compounds FEED-IS-UNINFORMATIVE
rather than excusing it: part of the feed's spread is in the target.

OPEN, AND IT QUALIFIES THE ABOVE. 163 route-years of circa 9,216, 1.8%, read sector below
pure P2P, which is impossible. RULER-SOUND found zero of 3,072 on the graded year, and the
difference is a basis one: the arm built both quantities from one source while this run
mixes preagg's od_p2p with the leg explosion computed here. A shortfall of a few passengers
is a rounding difference between two builds; a shortfall of thousands is a real mismatch.
The first run reported only the count, so the magnitude now goes into the why column. Re-run
and read it before the maturation medians are quoted as final.

A STATUS LINE OF MINE LIED, and it is the same shape as the three found in other files
today. This script printed "sector table present" unconditionally, because the line survived
from the version that exited when the table was absent. It fired on a store that has no
sector_adj at all, directly above the line reporting the leg explosion that only runs when
the table is missing. The two lines contradicted each other on screen. It now names which
path ran. Writing a status line before the code that decides the status is how this keeps
