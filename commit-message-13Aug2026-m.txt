The 163 are rounding, so the maturation result stands unqualified

Every flagged route-year reported a shortfall of median 0, p90 0 and max 0 passengers. So
sector equals pure P2P to floating point and the route carries no connecting traffic at all.
That is legitimate, and it is the same case RULER-SOUND found on 635 of 3,072 routes on the
graded year. Calling it impossible was my label rather than the data. preagg's od_p2p and
the leg explosion computed here therefore agree, and the maturation figures rest on one
basis after all.

MATURATION-REFUTED stands unqualified. Connecting-specific growth over the local leg is 2.6%
at Y2 and 2.9% at Y3, against the 10.2 a maturation explanation would have needed to close
RECUT-RESULT on its own. The grading year is not why the shipped feed over-reads.

THE COUNT ITSELF IS THE SMALLER FINDING. Two runs on identical inputs, same store and same
arm CSV, returned 163 and then 166. DuckDB sums in parallel and the order of a float
addition decides whether sector less P2P lands at exactly zero or a hair below it, so a
route flips between buckets run to run. That is the unordered-result trap of the
capture-share bug in a new costume: nothing is wrong with the data and the count is simply
not a stable quantity.

Fixed with a half-passenger tolerance. Below that is zero connecting traffic and is named as
such. A real shortfall still reports its size, because thousands would be a basis mismatch
between the two builds rather than a rounding difference, and that would put the whole
comparison in question. The summary line now says which of the two it found rather than
asserting either.
