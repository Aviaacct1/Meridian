Wire the calibrated model into route_forecast: the local leg, and seven rulings

Step 2 of RUNBOOK-WIRING, on the engine side. route_forecast.forecast takes a
p2p_demand_override which replaces the local demand leg. Nothing calls it yet, so no figure
moves.

THE THING THE RUNBOOK DID NOT KNOW, found by writing the wiring rather than by planning it.
Line 587 computes captured = market x share x dshare x stimulation, and that is the right
place for the model's number, but SEVEN further multipliers are applied to captured between
there and the plan cap. Each needed a ruling, and each comment in the file states the answer:

  market_factor      a trim learned to correct the QSI capture on large markets
  airport_capture    a per-airport correction from the engine's own past errors
  dest_thin_factor   corrects demand "under-allocated BY THE CATCHMENT MODEL"
  haul_trim          corrects the engine's haul bias, and gcd is a model feature
  freq_discount      corrects being "over-credited BY THE QSI SHARE"; freq is a feature
  induced floor      floors demand the engine under-reads on new markets
  bucket_correct     fitted on TOTAL error across the whole launch history

All seven are corrections to the QSI engine's own error and none of them transfers. BT2 is
trained on outturn directly, so applying them would be correcting one engine's bias on
another engine's number. All seven are suppressed when the model answers.

season_share is the only one applied. It is a property of the schedule being flown and the
model does not contain it.

THE YEAR BASIS, settled explicitly. Line 489 grows market to the forecast year before line
587, so captured is a forecast-year figure, while BT2 is anchored on base_mkt, the measured
O&D in the latest full year, so its output is current-year. The override is multiplied by
(1+growth)**growth_years and by nothing else. No separate maturity factor: connecting_
maturation measured the local leg at x1.013 from year one to year two and x1.104 to year
three, and market growth over two years accounts for most of that, so a route-specific ramp
is not separable from the growth already applied. Both would double count the same ten per
cent.

THE BUILD LIST IS REWRITTEN WITH THE ARITHMETIC. _bld is the table that goes on a slide, and
its four QSI steps describe a derivation that did not happen when the model produced the
local leg. The model path now prints the measured catchment market for context, the model's
own figure, and the growth carry-forward if there is one: three lines that are true instead
of four that are not.

ONE COST, RECORDED RATHER THAN WORKED AROUND. bucket_correct multiplies the feed as well as
captured, so suppressing it also removes the feed's share. Applying half of an old fit to
half of a new number would be worse. The way back is to refit bucket_correct on whichever
engine ships.
