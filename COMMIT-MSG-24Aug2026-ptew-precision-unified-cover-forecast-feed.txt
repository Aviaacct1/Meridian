PTEW: one calculation, one precision, across Cover, Forecast and Connecting feed
(Jol Kingham, 24 Aug 2026, "SJC: PTEWs inconsistency")

Jol's email: "Connecting feed 2-way" H22 (35.6) + H43 (111.8) = 147.4. "Forecast 2-way"
and "Cover" show 148, not 147. Is that ok?

Not "ok" by the standard set earlier today (every PTEW figure across every tab should
foot and agree, not merely round close). Root cause was different from the earlier
footing bugs: the Forecast tab's PTEW column rounded to a WHOLE NUMBER
(round(x/(freq*weeks))), while Cover and the Connecting feed tabs already showed 1
decimal place. Summing two values independently rounded from 1dp to 0dp, then comparing
against the 1dp answer, produced a genuine, expected difference (behind 35.611 rounds to
36 at 0dp but 35.6 at 1dp; beyond 111.78 rounds to 112 at 0dp but 111.8 at 1dp;
36+112=148, 35.6+111.8=147.4->147).

Two changes in app/cortex_workbook.py:

1. Forecast tab's ptew() lambda bumped from round(x, 0) to round(x, 1), matching the
   precision already used everywhere else PTEW appears. All four PTEW cell formats on
   that tab (fmt="#,##0" -> fmt="#,##0.0") updated to match. The footing logic itself
   (grand total = sum of parts; sub-rows = remainder against their leg) is unchanged,
   just operating at finer granularity.

2. Cover's own PTEW figure no longer comes from dem["pdew_total"], a figure computed
   independently in cortex_app.py from the same freq/weeks basis but via a completely
   separate code path. freq and the ptew() lambda are now defined once, at the top of
   build_workbook(), before Cover is built, and Cover's PTEW is the identical footed sum
   (ptew(p2p) + ptew(behind) + ptew(beyond)) the Forecast tab's GRAND TOTAL row prints.
   Two tabs computing "the same" figure via two different formulas is exactly the class
   of problem closed out three times already today - this removes the second formula
   entirely rather than patching it to agree more closely. cortex_app.py's own
   pdew_total is untouched and still used by the dashboard and deck, which this change
   does not touch.

Verification: test_workbook_table.py's Cover-matches-Forecast check tightened from a
