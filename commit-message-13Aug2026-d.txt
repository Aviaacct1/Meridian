Record that the 2015-2017 region gap is a loader question, not a missing-data one

Handover item 6 says to load the Africa and Middle East workbooks from Egnyte for those
years. They are already there, and the item as written buys data Avia already owns.

WHAT IS ON EGNYTE. /Shared/Company Data/18 Products/QSI/Data Store holds monthly region
files, and for Africa and Middle East those start at 2018: a filename search for "Aug 2015"
returns exactly five, Asia, Europe, Latin America, North America and Southwest Pacific,
which is precisely the five regions the 2015-08 label carries. The subfolder old\ holds the
same regions for the missing years as HALF-YEAR files:

  Africa H1 2015  222MB     Africa H2 2015  242MB
  Africa H1 2016  231MB     Africa H2 2016  248MB
  Africa H1 2017  257MB     Africa H2 2017  276MB
  Africa H1 2018  271MB     Africa H2 2018  309MB

So the coverage measurement was right about the monthly labels and wrong about the world.

THE MECHANISM IS ONE LINE. oag_ingest_periodic.py line 133 reads os.listdir on the folder
and keeps only names ending .xlsx, .xls or .xlsm, so a subfolder is returned as a name,
fails the extension filter and is dropped in silence. The default folder is the Data Store
root, so nothing in old\ is loaded by a default run. Not proven that they were never
loaded, because the script carries an --only flag whose help text uses "Africa H1" as its
worked example. The store is the arbiter and this commit does not pretend to settle it.

AND IF THEY ARE LOADED, THE LEG QUERY STILL CANNOT READ THEM. oag_ingest_periodic line 9
maps "Middle East H1 2015.xlsx" to the period key 2015-H1. bt2_capture_core.load_legs
queries one label with week=? and builds its window as {mon}-15 to {mon}-21, so on 2017-H1
that is "2017-H1-15", which is not a date. It is the same failure ONE-IMPLEMENTATION
recorded for a single-week label and refused by name, arriving from the other direction.
bt2_profile line 37 already counts the case as half_year_only_AF_ME and excludes those
routes rather than reading them.

THE CONTROL BEFORE ANY FIX. Rows carry eff_from and eff_to, so selecting the week of the
15th to the 21st out of a half-year label is the same query with a different label and the
same date window. Africa exists in BOTH forms for 2018. Slice 15 to 21 August 2018 from the
monthly label and from the half-year label and compare distinct flights at JNB and CAI. If
they agree the fallback is sound and 2015-2017 is recoverable without buying anything. If
they do not, an OAG half-year pull states the schedule as filed at download rather than as
filed each month, and we know that before rebuilding six cohorts.
