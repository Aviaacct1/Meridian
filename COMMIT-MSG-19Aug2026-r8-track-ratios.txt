R8: Sabre-graded track record rows show band, ratio and verdict, no volumes

The per-route tables printed forecast and carried volumes for every launch.
DB1B-graded US rows are public domain and keep both. Sabre-graded rows now
show a size band (fixed edges, 1k to 1m), the graded ratio to one decimal
place, and the verdict; neither volume prints.

The finding that shaped it, caught by the render check rather than reasoned
in advance: on the calibrated evidence file the fitted forecast reproduces
the outturn to a part in a thousand on nearly half the routes, so printing
the model's own forecast volume IS printing the Sabre outturn by another
name (five identical renderings in one BUD table, measured). The first cut
of this fix kept the forecast column and was wrong; both absolutes come off
for Sabre-graded rows.

- app/track_record.py: load_rows carries outturn_source and a dot_graded
  flag per row; the route table renders per grading source; headers and the
  table note state the rule and the reason.

Verified: render check on a Sabre-graded airport (BUD): zero volume strings
from either column; DB1B airport (BNA) keeps 24 carried volumes; bands and
40 ratio cells render; test_track_control 11 and test_attribution 28 checks
still 0 failed.

With this, the Thursday demo no longer needs to steer around any airport on
the track record page.
