THE CELL ANSWERS IT. Long haul and a named Asian or Gulf hub, n=70: median 0.235, IQR 0.122
to 0.628, k needed 0.235, in band 8.6%. It is the best cell in the table on both the level
and the band, and it is still 4.26 times below the shipped 1.0. Allowing the FLOOR-EVIDENCED
long-haul 2.50x under-count takes it to 0.587, short by 1.70x. So SJC-TPE sits inside this
population, not outside it, and the live path agreeing with the 2025 analyst at k=1.0 is not
explained by the route being an unusual type.

I OVERSTATED SHAPE-CONFIRMED AND AM WITHDRAWING THE STRENGTH OF IT, not its direction.
Holding haul constant, the hub effect shrinks:

  within long haul    0.235 against 0.192, a factor of 1.22 (n=70 against 379)
  within short haul   0.118 against 0.068, a factor of 1.74 (n=114 against 1,328)
  reported marginal   0.181 against 0.090, a factor of 2.01

The marginal figure sits above both conditional ones because the hub group is 38% long haul
against the non-hub group's 22%, so it carries the mix. FLOOR-EVIDENCED's 2.07x hub and
2.50x long haul were also separate marginal cuts and carry the same confound. The two agree
on a quantity they both confound the same way, which is not the independent corroboration I
claimed this morning. Direction and rough size stand.

THE CANDIDATE THAT IS MATERIAL AND NOT YET MEASURED is GRADE-BASIS arriving on the
connecting side. Meridian forecasts a maturity year: forecast_year defaults to the base data
year plus one and every client case runs 2027 or 2028. The arm grades at --offset 1, the
route's first full year, when an airline has not built or sold its connecting itineraries.
If connecting traffic matures over two or three years then outturn_pax less p2p_outturn at
L+1 is systematically short of what a maturity-year forecast should show, and the ruler is
wrong rather than the feed. Testable in one 45-minute run at --offset 2, and it bears on the
P2P leg and the published accuracy as much as on the feed.

MY RECOMMENDATION IS NOT TO MOVE k. The spread survives every cut: the best cell puts 8.6%
of routes within +-20% and Europe and North America put 0.0%, against an IQR spanning a
factor of seventeen. Changing the level would move every client number, including the
SJC-TPE figures that reconcile with the analyst, in exchange for re-centring a distribution
that still does not describe the route in front of you. Settle the grading year first, then
decide the level, and nothing about the connecting leg changes on a client deliverable until
both are done.
