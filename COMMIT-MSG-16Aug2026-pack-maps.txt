Forecast pack: the catchment and route maps that were never wired (Job 2 close)

The 14 August corrections list was already largely in the committed code (the
resolver, the Observatory disclaimer, the load factor label, the code columns,
the year headings, the slide-32 pre-stimulation column and its six notes); this
verifies those by reading and test, and builds the two pieces that never
existed: the catchment page with population at each end, and the route map.

- cortex_app.catchment_profile: the body of /api/catchment split into a
  callable, one definition for the portal page and the deck contract, so the
  two can never show different catchments for one airport. The endpoint's
  behaviour is unchanged; it now wraps the function.
- deck_from_cases computes a profile per route end (cached per airport per
  invocation) and passes catchment_ends into the contract. A failed profile is
  carried as ok=False with its reason, never thinned.
- forecast_to_contract writes catchment.ends into the contract; a failed end
  becomes _ends_partial, both failed becomes _ends_need, and no profiles at all
  names where they come from. Flag, never fill.
- avia_maps.catchment_end_map: the generalised per-end map, drawn from the
  profile rather than by hand (catchment_map stays as the legacy SJC plate):
  locales coloured by drive band and sized by population, airport marked, the
  largest places named, grey for a locale the engine could not route.
- forecast_pack: one catchment page per end (map, population by drive band,
  measured capture where held), falling back to the population table without
  basemap and to the zones table without ends, the reason stated; a route page
  with the great circle and the schedule beneath it, dropped without its map
  because the schedule already lives on the methodology page; render_maps draws
  the images beside the output before the spec references them.
- build_pack assembly rebuilt: pages carry their NAMES and a page function may
  return a list, so the dropped-list can no longer be misnamed by adding a page
  (the old version detected drops by slicing at fixed indices).
- test_forecast_pack_maps.py: 19 fixture checks, no stores needed: the ends
  through the contract with every failure shape named, the pre-stimulation
  derivation clearing its need note, growth as total not CAGR, connecting legs
  at x1.00, the pages and drops in every maps/no-maps combination, and a real
  pptx render. Maps drawn for real where basemap is installed and skipped with
  a statement where it is not. All existing suites still pass.

Owed on the workstation: deck_from_cases cases_sjc_tpe.json --report-only to
see the ends fill from the real GeoNames dump and drive engine; a full pack
render for SJC-TPE to eyeball both catchment maps and the route page; a smoke
check of /api/catchment after the refactor. The competition-split rows stay
open by design: they are coupled to the Job 3 gap measurement and need John's
call, not an overnight build.
