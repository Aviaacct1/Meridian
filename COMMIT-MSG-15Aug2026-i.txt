The 5.36% reconciled, and the fare basis stopped from moving with the volume source

THE MARKET MOVEMENT IS EXPLAINED AND IT IS A GAIN. TPA-AUS decomposed: coverage
gross-up 0.930 under Sabre against 1.000 under DOT, natural market 368,074 against
387,789. coverage.py line 27 sets the US country factor at 0.93, so it is a gross-DOWN
and not a gross-up: Sabre reads US markets slightly rich and the engine scales them
back. Removing it multiplies by 1/0.93. Arithmetic on the run's own figures:
387,789 / 368,074 = 1.0536, and 1.0536 x 0.93 = 0.9799, which is the 0.989x
dot_ratio_check measured on the total quantity once the index factor and the different
pair scope are allowed for. THE CHAIN CLOSES and there is nothing unexplained in it.

That makes the movement an accuracy argument rather than a discrepancy. coverage.py's
own docstring records the US factor as fitted at n=4 and "heavily shrunk", so a US route
on DOT retires a four-route correction and replaces it with the census. That is the
sentence to put to Tampa.

THE DEFECT FOUND IN THE SAME OUTPUT. Average fare fell 19.0%, 229.58 to 186.06, because
DB1B's MktFare excludes taxes and fees while Sabre's avg_total_fare_usd includes them.
They are two quantities under one field name, which is the shape of all four contract
defects of 14 August. Every revenue and yield figure in the deck runs off avg_fare, so
turning the switch on would have cut deck revenue by a fifth for a reason having nothing
to do with revenue, on exactly the runs the switch exists to improve.
