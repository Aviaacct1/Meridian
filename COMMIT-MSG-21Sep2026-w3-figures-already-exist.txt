W3 status: the pitch page's three figures already exist, so it is wiring not invention

John asked W3 to check the SJC-TPE work before reinventing it. W3's session-1
status said the route map, the time-of-day curve and the tail chart "do not exist
in the pack at all". True of the pack, wrong about the codebase, and the
difference is days of work.

The time-of-day curve is built, wired and live: cortex_workbook.render_curve_png,
matplotlib rather than openpyxl's native chart because shaded restricted-hour
bands and an annotated callout cannot be done in the Excel chart model without
hand-editing chart XML, rendered before wb.save() and embedded in the Departure
curve sheet beside its own data table and a native Excel line chart.

The route and catchment maps are built and wired: forecast_pack.render_maps
through avia_maps.route_map, both route ends, reached from demo_pack.py.

The tail chart is built: drawTail and tailPattern in cortex_dashboard.html, with
the day-of-week seam marked, in the language the pitch page is written in.

The workbook is eight sheets with each-way and two-way pairs, method notes and
source lines throughout, and Avia Solutions already set as creator and
last-modified-by.

The status records one judgement W3 will make unless the controller rules
otherwise: where a figure carries numbers, the pitch page uses the proven
renderers rather than a second interactive chart of the same run, because two
pictures of one run that disagree would breach the download-fidelity ruling.
Interactivity is kept for the economics sliders, which already exist and work.
