Refuse an impossible months and launch month pair, and fix the two callers that used one

Step 1 of RUNBOOK-WIRING, and a real defect whether or not the model is ever wired.

bt2_gbm.X_of feeds the model log(months) as feature six and month_num as feature thirteen.
In training those two are perfectly collinear: bt2_profile counts months from the launch
month to year end, so months_operated = 13 - launch_month in every one of 6,810 rows.

route_context.build defaulted to months=12 with launch_mon=6, a pair that occurs zero times
in training and cannot occur by construction, since all 192 rows carrying twelve months
launched in January. It now refuses an impossible pair BY NAME, the way load_legs refuses a
single-week OAG label, and the default moves to launch_mon=1, which is the January case and
the only place a twelve-month operation exists on the manifold. The rule enforced is
months <= 13 - launch_mon, which is bt2_profile's own construction.

Tested on ten cases including both defaults, both boundary launches and the out-of-range
ones. The old default is refused with the reason and the maximum stated.

AND TWO CALLERS CARRIED THE SAME PAIR, found by looking for what the guard would break.
route_context's own __main__ passed launch_mon=6 against the months default of 12 and would
now stop; it is fixed to months=7, which is what a June launch means. compare_engines.py had
--months 12.0 with --launch-month 6 as its COMMAND LINE defaults, so every comparison anyone
has run from it fed the model an impossible pair.

So the off-manifold call was not hypothetical. It was the default on the one tool built to
compare the two engines. Both are fixed and the argparse help now states the rule.
