Floor A/B instrument (item 6): --no-split-floor alone now changes only the floor

The floor question is decided by one A/B on the pinned set, and the instrument
for it was confounded: --no-split-floor built the feed-fix cfg, and any truthy
feed_cfg also zeroed P2P carriers' feed, swapped the behind base capture onto
behind_cap and put _cap_eff onto the dominance path. An arm meant to differ
from the shipped control by the floor differed by four things and would have
called the sum of them the floor.

- route_feed._fix_on: the one question every "is the fix on" site now asks,
  instead of bool(cfg). A cfg carrying _floor_only is not the fix. Fixed at
  the four sites: _cap_eff, the P2P zeroing in feed_side and behind_feed, and
  the behind base capture / dominance reads in behind_feed.
- backtest.py: --no-split-floor without --feed-fix/--mct-banking/--qsi-feed
  builds {"split_floor": False, "_floor_only": True} and prints it, so the
  arm's console states what ran. Combined with those flags, behaviour is
  unchanged from before.
- test_floor_only_arm.py: 13 fixture checks: _fix_on on all three shapes,
  _cap_eff flat on the floor-only cfg and scaling under the fix, conn_coeff
  identical either way, the floor read on all three shapes, and the P2P
  zeroing gated off the floor-only arm (using a carrier actually in the set).
  test_qsi_feed still passes.
