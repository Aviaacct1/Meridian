The departure time now chooses itself, per airline, and it drives the demand

Three things, and the third is the one that was asked for.

1. THE QSI FEED WAS SCORING TAIPEI CONNECTIONS AT A QUARTER TO ONE IN THE MORNING

qsi_feed.beyond_capture computed the arrival at the connecting hub as
(dep_time_mins + flying_mins) % 1440. That adds block time to the ORIGIN's local departure
and reads the answer as the HUB's local clock. On SJC-TPE it put China Airlines on the
ground at Taipei at 00:45 instead of 16:45, a sixteen-hour error, while every competing
leg came off the OAG board correctly in local time from local_arr_time. The new route was
the only mistimed itinerary in its own competition.

Eleven beyond markets carrying 37,785 passengers each way, 6.2% of the beyond base, scored
zero for being "over MAX_CONNECT". The real connections are two to three hours: CA to
Chongqing departs 19:20 against a 16:45 arrival, Etihad to Abu Dhabi 19:40, MU to Wuhan
18:40. All were read as thirteen to nineteen hours. This is also the explanation for the
QSI feed anti-correlating with the 2025 analyst at Pearson -0.46, which the handover
recorded as a possible disagreement about method. It was not a disagreement about method.

_hub_arrival_mins applies the timezone shift, using the same longitude approximation
cortex_app._schedule_times uses, so the bank is scored against the arrival time on the page
rather than a second estimate of it. Verified: SJC-TPE returns 16:45, matching the engine's
own schedule payload.

2. THE DEPARTURE TIME IS NOW AN INPUT TO THE DEMAND

It was not one before, anywhere. _schedule_times placed the outbound at 11:00, ran AFTER
the forecast and only decorated the payload, so no time of day could move a passenger.
/api/optimise swept airline, aircraft, frequency, carrier type and season; departure time
was not a dimension. Measured across the day on SJC-TPE the beyond capture runs 0.98% to
5.43% and the behind capture 0.31% to 2.17%, so the placeholder was choosing the answer,
and choosing it badly: no carrier flies an 11:00 departure from the Bay Area to Taipei.

route_feed.optimise_departure searches the day on connecting passengers won across BOTH
sides, because one departure time drives both: it sets which behind-origin feeders can
make the flight, and with the block it sets which beyond-hub bank the aircraft connects
into. Both markets are built once and held across the sweep. Coarse grid then a
refinement, settable with AVIA_DEP_STEP and AVIA_DEP_REFINE.

calibrated_forecast takes dep_time_mins. Named, it is honoured, which is the client
testing their own schedule. Left blank with an airline named, it is optimised FOR THAT
AIRLINE, because an airline connects online onto its own onward legs and only interlines
onto everyone else's. Cached per route and operator: block time comes from distance rather
than the aircraft, and frequency enters the score identically at every time of day, so
both move the level and not the choice.

3. RESTRICTED HOURS, AND THE COST OF THEM IS QUOTED

The best connecting time is very often exactly the time the airport cannot take. San Jose
is the case: the whole Taipei market leaves the west coast between midnight and two in the
morning, which is inside the night restriction. optimise_departure takes `restricted`, one
or more windows in the origin's local time ("23:00-06:00"), exposed as restricted_hours on
calibrated_forecast and as AVIA_RESTRICTED_HOURS.

It screens MOVEMENTS, not departures. A curfew stops the aircraft coming back as surely as
it stops it leaving, so the return arrival is screened too; it falls out as dep + 2 x block
+ turnaround because the timezone shift cancels between the legs. The window boundaries are
tested explicitly as candidates, because an optimum against a constraint lies on the
constraint: an airline schedules right up against a curfew rather than near it.

The unrestricted optimum is always computed and returned beside the permitted one, so the
COST of the restriction is a number rather than an absence. SJC-TPE, China Airlines, 4x:

  no restriction              departs 00:30, arrives TPE 06:15
  SJC 23:00-06:00             departs 06:30, arrives TPE 12:15, returns SJC 12:00
  cost of the restriction     42,655 connecting passengers two-way a year

That figure is the argument an airport takes to a curfew review, and it is worth more than
the forecast it came out of.
