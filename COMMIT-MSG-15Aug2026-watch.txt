Route Watch and the Observatory briefing: the monitoring option's skeleton

The +5k monitoring tier from the 15 August pricing note, version one, honest
about its basis on every block.

- app/route_watch.py: capacity moves at an airport (new, dropped, changed
  services at >=10%, out and in, competitors beside them), latest weekly OAG
  snapshot against the nearest year-earlier snapshot, chosen by DATE not
  string order (the OAG-WEEK trap); the gap is reported when it is not a
  clean year. Demand trend by Sabre vintage, two-way, with the
  SOURCE-YEAR-2021 caveat in the basis line. Every block states that the
  store is a static extract, not a live feed: continuous weeks are a data
  subscription decision. Known-answer tested on a synthetic store: new /
  dropped / changed classify exactly, monthly-form labels are ignored.
- app/news_brief.py: five curated news items for the airport's route
  development team via the existing Claude research pipeline. One live call
  per airport per day, cached under LOCAL_CACHE's parent (never the repo);
  an item without a source and URL is dropped rather than shown; a missing
  API key refuses clearly instead of rendering an empty panel.
- app/cortex_watch.html + /watch page, /api/watch and /api/briefing in
  cortex_app; Route Watch added to the dashboard nav.
- _db_paths: the stale C:\Avia literal fallback (pre-git Dev PC folder,
  exists on no current machine) now falls back to config's per-machine
  store paths; the env vars still win. Same stale-default shape the review
  closed elsewhere.

Post-pull check (workstation): open /watch, load a real airport, confirm
the two snapshot labels read sensibly and the demand vintages match the
airport profile. The briefing button needs ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and costs one
research call per airport per day.

Files: app/route_watch.py, app/news_brief.py, app/cortex_watch.html,
app/cortex_app.py, app/cortex_dashboard.html
