app/od_source.py
  feed_market(): the connecting market for one feed side, reading DB1B MktCoupons = 2,
  which is the same itinerary shape as Sabre's connecting_airport1 IS NOT NULL AND
  connecting_airport2 IS NULL. A feed scope is rarely one country, so rather than
  refuse the side because one point is foreign, the scope is PARTITIONED: all-US pairs
  from DB1B, the rest from Sabre, merged, with the DOT share returned so a slide can
  state what it actually read. Returns (market, source, dot_share).
  _year_complete(): a year whose four quarters are not all logged built is refused
  outright. 2016 is short Q1, and three quarters read as four under-states every market
  in the year by circa a quarter, which is a silent gap-fill in the other direction.
  _us(): airportsdata loaded once rather than per call, a feed scope being hundreds of
  points.
  factor_indirect is applied to the DOT figures IDENTICALLY to the Sabre ones. It is a
  Sabre-era constant from the 2013 BA method and may not belong on DOT data, but that is
  a second measurement. Swapping the source changes one thing or nothing afterwards is
  attributable.

app/route_feed.py
  feed_side (beyond) and behind_feed (behind) now take their market through
  od_source.feed_market, with the existing Sabre or preagg query passed in as the
  fallback so neither path is duplicated and preagg still works. The source label and
  DOT share are written back to feed_cfg as _beyond_source, _beyond_dot_share,
  _behind_source and _behind_dot_share, for the payload and contract work in piece 2.

bt2/od_source_feed_check.py (new)
  Six cases on a synthetic store, 6 of 6 pass. The first is the one that protects the
  rest of the engine: with the switch off, the FULL scope is handed to the Sabre path
  and its answer is returned unchanged. Also asserted: an all-US scope reads entirely
  from DB1B with the factor applied and the nonstop class never read; a mixed scope
  splits US to DOT and foreign to Sabre with the share correct at 0.905; a short year
  falls back; and the SJC-TPE shape, where the far end of every pair is foreign, falls
  back.
