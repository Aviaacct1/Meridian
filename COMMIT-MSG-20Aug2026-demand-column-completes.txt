Demand column completes on the All-other row, everywhere the connecting-city table renders

John's finding, checking the EVA pack: the forecast column completes to the carried
leg on the All-other row, but demand stayed blank. 19 August's note said market demand
was "a different quantity" from the leg and would be papered over by a filler. Checked
against the pipeline and that was wrong: catchment_headline.connecting_market_over_hub
/_destination (or, in the raw payload, dem["feed_beyond_base"]/["feed_behind_base"]) is
the full uncapped market before capture, the SAME source the shown cities' own
annual_demand figures are drawn from, additive with them, each way like the city rows
(the forecast leg is two-way in the PPTX/Excel surfaces; this file's own HTML report
stays each-way throughout, so no doubling there). Reconciles exactly to the numbers
Jol first queried on 19 August (181,565 old vs 181,551 now, the difference being the
already-logged small run-to-run drift, not a new defect).
