Filenames carry airline, aircraft and frequency, not just the route

John, 22 August: every download for a given route shared one base name -
"Meridian_SJC_TPE.xlsx" whether it was CI on an A359 5x/week or BR on a 777 3x/week
- so the only thing telling two configurations apart on disk was the browser's own
"(1)", "(2)" counter, meaningless once you've pulled more than a couple.

app/cortex_app.py, both places a download filename is built (the standard
deck/workbook/csv/zip download around line 2767, and the pitch-job build around
line 2852): airline, aircraft and frequency now ride in the base name, e.g.
Meridian_SJC_TPE_CI_A359_5x.xlsx. A blank airline (new-entrant scenarios with no
carrier set) is dropped from the tag rather than printed as an empty segment, so
the filename degrades gracefully instead of reading "Meridian_SJC_TPE__A359_5x".

Not touched: the demo lead-capture pack's filename (a separate, single-recipient,
watermarked artifact, not something compared side by side the way pitch downloads
are) - flag if that should carry the same tag.
