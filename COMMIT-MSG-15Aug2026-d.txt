dot_ratio_check: DOT against Sabre on nonstop, connecting and total

app/dot_ratio_check.py (new)

WHAT IT IS FOR. DEFAULT_CONN_CAPTURE and behind_cap were calibrated against
Sabre-measured markets. od_source now reads DB1B for the all-US pairs of a feed scope,
so the moment AVIA_OD_SOURCE moves off sabre the feed forecast moves by the
DB1B-to-Sabre ratio on those pairs, before any method changes. That ratio has never
been measured on the connecting quantity, because until 15 August the connecting
quantity did not exist in the DOT store.

Compares matched pairs for one year on three quantities: nonstop (MktCoupons = 1
against Sabre's connecting_airport1 IS NULL), connecting (MktCoupons = 2 against the
live single-stop filter) and total. Neither side is adjusted: no factor_indirect, no
capture, no grossing beyond the x10 already in the store.

Pairs present in one source and not the other are counted and reported separately
rather than dropped, because a coverage difference IS the measurement here.

Refuses a year whose four quarters are not all logged built, the same guard as
od_source._year_complete, so 2016 cannot be compared as though it were whole.

Prints the connecting ratio against the nonstop ratio, which is the finding: near 1
means Sabre's coverage gap is flat and capture can be re-levelled by one number; above
1 means Sabre under-reads TRANSFER traffic specifically, which is a different
correction and bears on the 19% gap against the 2025 analyst.

TESTED on synthetic stores built to a known answer: nonstop level and connecting
under-read 2x recovers 1.000x, 2.000x and 2.000x exactly, lists the diverging pairs,
and reports the DOT-only and Sabre-only pairs rather than silently dropping them. The
short-year guard refuses a three-quarter year.
