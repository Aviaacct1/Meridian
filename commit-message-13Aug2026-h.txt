The feed level over-reads circa tenfold, the floor's shape is corroborated, and the spread is the finding

Measured on 1,891 routes of backtest_qsifeed_11Aug2026.csv re-levelled from k=0.06 to the
shipped k=1.0. No engine run.

VALIDATION FIRST. LEVEL-VS-SHAPE recorded the QSI feed needing x1.62 at k=0.06, which
implies 0.0972 at k=1.0. The tool returns 0.098, a difference of 0.8%. So this is the same
population read a different way rather than a new claim.

ALL ROUTES: median actual over feed 0.098, IQR 0.020 to 0.348, k needed 0.098.

THE FLOOR'S DESIGN IS INDEPENDENTLY CORROBORATED, and it is the positive finding.

  by haul      0.062 (n=889, 1500-3000km)   0.083 (n=553)   0.196 (n=449, over 6000km)
               a factor of 3.16 short to long
  by hub       0.090 (n=1,707) to 0.181 (n=184) on a named Asian or Gulf destination,
               a factor of 2.01, against the 2.07x FLOOR-EVIDENCED measured on outturn by a
               completely different route
  by carrier   FSC 0.114 against LCC 0.052, a factor of 2.19, the same story again

Two independent measurements of one connecting under-count agreeing to within 3%.

AND THE SHIPPED LEVEL STILL DOES NOT STAND. No cut of any size approaches 1.0. The highest
is 0.196 on 449 long-haul routes, so even where the level suits best it is 5.1 times too
high. Europe reads 0.017 on 197 routes, 59 times too high, and North America 0.063 on 187.
Cohorts are flat at 0.073 to 0.147, so this is not a vintage effect. Allowing the full
FLOOR-EVIDENCED 2.07x under-count in the grading quantity moves the hub cut to circa 0.36,
still short by a factor of three. "Right for the case it was tuned on" is refuted: it is
wrong everywhere and less wrong on long haul into a hub.

THE SPREAD IS THE FINDING AND TUNING k WOULD BE THE WRONG WORK. The IQR runs 0.020 to
0.348, a factor of seventeen across the middle half, and the in-band column never exceeds
6.5% on any cut: 2.6% on 1500-3000km, 5.1% on long haul, 6.5% on the hub list, 0.0% on
Europe and on North America. At any single value of k fewer than one route in fifteen lands
within +-20% of its actual connecting traffic. Choosing k is choosing where to centre a
distribution that does not describe the individual route.
