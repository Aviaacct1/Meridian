Plan LF: measured, and withdraw the transfer correction that was never measured

LF-CAP-OPEN answered on 2,871 launches. Haul does not move achieved load factor:
medians 0.771, 0.790, 0.777 and 0.782 across the four cuts, a range of 1.9 points,
so per-haul variation of the cap is not supported. Carrier type does, and it
survives holding haul: ULCC above FSC by 4 to 6 points in all three cuts with
enough routes to read. The shipped 0.875 sits at the 85.5th percentile of what
launches actually achieve, which is where a cap belongs, so it stands.

Withdraws the transfer-correction caveat this file shipped with yesterday. It
said the Sabre numerator reads low by the 2.07x and 2.50x of FLOOR-EVIDENCED.
FLOOR-EVIDENCED measures "the multiplier the FLAT feed needs to reach" actual
connecting, with the Sabre residual as the target: it is a measurement of the
engine's under-read, not of Sabre's coverage. Applying it as a Sabre correction
is arithmetically impossible on this population. Median connecting share of the
sector on 336 long-haul launches is 0.572, so at 2.50x the median achieved load
factor goes from 0.782 to 1.360 with 78% of launches above 100%, and at 2.07x to
1.185 with 71%. The docstring and the printed output now carry the refutation and
the run that produced it.

The control's exclusions are now written to plan_lf_achieved_excluded.csv with
the reason on each row. Previously the run reported a count of 155 impossible
launches and the worst case and kept the rows, so the filter could not be audited
and the charter-market hypothesis for them could not be checked.
