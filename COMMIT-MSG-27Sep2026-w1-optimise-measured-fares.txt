W1: Optimise sizes and ranks aircraft on the market's measured cabin fares, not the distance proxy

  premium share) on every payload; Optimise stage 1 passes them to stage 2 and to the carrier
  check, and select_aircraft prices economy and premium seats at them. The distance proxy
  (max(180, nm x 0.11)) is used only when the market has no measured fare; a premium fare the
  caller set still wins.

- routes/W1-STATUS.md: cabin-fare acceptance recorded.

on long haul (SOU-JFK proxy circa 330 USD against 935 measured), so the ranking and the economics
screen disagreed. Passenger numbers per schedule are unchanged; which schedule Optimise picks may
change, so the four-pair Optimise acceptance is re-run.
