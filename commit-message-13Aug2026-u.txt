Measure the level the engine's local leg needs, and whether a level can fix it at all

bt2/local_level_fit.py is the better-posed job the cross-check pointed at. It measures the
multiplier the local leg needs to reach outturn, captured_uncapped against p2p_outturn, both
from the same arm row at the same graded year. No seats denominator, no join, no year
mismatch: the three things that invalidated the cross-check twice. All 3,072 arm rows are
available rather than the 730 that survived the join.

THREE DISCIPLINES BUILT IN, each one paid for earlier in the programme.

  Cuts are reported as a HAUL x TYPE cross-tab as well as singly, because
  HUB-EFFECT-IS-MOSTLY-HAUL showed a marginal cut carrying another cut's mix: a hub effect of
  x2.01 on the margin fell to x1.22 inside long haul.

  Every multiplier is learned leave one cohort out, with a 30-route minimum before a segment
  is used at all and a fall back to the global number below it.

  A CEILING is reported: the band if every cut's median were corrected perfectly on its own
  routes. That is fitted and unachievable, and it is the most a level correction of this
  shape can ever buy.

THE CEILING IS THE POINT, and the reading is fixed in advance. RECUT-RESULT and
FEED-IS-UNINFORMATIVE both ended in the same place: a level cannot fix a spread. So the
question is not what multiplier the long-haul local leg needs, which is already known to be
circa 1.6, but whether applying it moves the band. If the ceiling sits close to the
uncorrected figure, the local leg is not off by a level and no multiplier will rescue it.

Checked on synthetic populations with known answers before touching the arm. A pure level
fault with a tight spread recovers 47.8% to 74.1% against a ceiling of 73.9%. A spread fault
goes 15.6% to 16.8% against a ceiling of 16.9%. The script separates the two cases, and the
leave-one-cohort-out figure tracking the ceiling means it is not overfitting.

AND IT HAS THE CONTROL THE CROSS-CHECK LACKED. The arm already carries fc_over_p2p, so the
ratio computed here is checked against it row by row and the run stops if more than one per
cent disagree by more than 2%. Worth noting that the cross-check's first two runs would both
have passed such a check, because its fault was in a denominator brought from elsewhere
rather than in the arm's own columns. Prefer a left and right hand side from one row wherever
the measurement allows it.
