dot_ratio_check: US-to-US by default, and the cut that governs the feed

Two corrections to my own tool, both found by reading its first real output.

THE FIRST RUN'S SABRE-ONLY FIGURE WAS NOT A COVERAGE GAP. On pairs touching SJC, TPA
and ORD in 2024 the tool reported 6,031 Sabre-only pairs carrying 14.2m passengers,
against 2,199 matched pairs. The pair selection was "either end is one of the named
airports" with no US test, so the Sabre side carried ORD-LHR and every other
international pair, which DB1B cannot hold by construction. Those present as DB1B
blindness and are nothing of the kind. The comparison is now restricted to US-to-US
pairs, using the coupon store as its own US list: DB1B is domestic only, so every
airport in it is a US airport and no external table is needed. --all-pairs keeps the
old behaviour as a switch rather than as the default.

STATED LIMIT, because it cuts the wrong way. That US list is "every airport DB1B has
ever seen", which excludes the commuter and EAS tail DB1B is blind to, and those are
exactly the pairs od_source's auto mode falls back to Sabre for. So the filter removes
some genuine coverage gap along with the international noise, and the Sabre-only
figure is now a floor rather than the whole of it.

THE AGGREGATE WAS THE WRONG STATISTIC FOR THE QUESTION. The first run returned a
connecting ratio of 1.047x while its ten widest divergences were all dense nonstop
pairs at 2.6x to 4.2x, so most pairs must agree and the movement is concentrated
somewhere. The feed legs never read a dense pair's connecting market: they read
feeder-to-destination markets, which are pairs people connect over BECAUSE there is
little or no nonstop. The tool now cuts the connecting ratio by the pair's DOT nonstop
share, into no nonstop at all, under 10%, 10 to 50%, and 50% or more. The first of
those is the population the feed actually reads. Same shape as HAUL-IS-A-TAIL-EFFECT:
the statistic has to match the question, and a median or an aggregate rarely does.

Tested on the synthetic stores: an international pair added to the Sabre side is
excluded by default and restored by --all-pairs, and the cuts recover the constructed
2.000x in each populated cut.
