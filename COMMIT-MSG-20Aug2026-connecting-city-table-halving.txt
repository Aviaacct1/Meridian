Connecting-city detail table: halve the now-two-way market total before it meets
the each-way city rows

Second-order consequence of this morning's connecting-basis fix, caught by
checking the three regenerated packs before they went back to Mark, not by a
fresh finding on a call.

deck/forecast_pack.py's _connecting() (the "Passengers connecting at TPE/SJC"
city-detail pages) completes the demand column's All-other/Total rows from
route_metadata.catchment_headline.connecting_market_over_hub/_destination. Until
this morning that field was each way, additive with the printed cities' own
each-way "annual_demand" figures with no conversion needed - correct at the time.
The connecting-basis fix doubled it to two-way at the source (Deck Generator/
deck_contract.py), to fix the mix Jol caught on the summary page. This page's
own arithmetic was never updated to match, so it went on subtracting an each-way
subtotal (the fifteen shown cities) from a now-two-way total: on the regenerated
China Airlines pack, "All other connecting markets" printed 901,037 and "Total"
printed 1,438,972 for the TPE leg, both roughly double the true each-way figures,
confirmed against the fifteen printed cities summing to 537,935 each way.

Fixed the same way the forecast column already handles this exact shape: leg_ew
halves the two-way carried figure two lines below, so mkt_leg now takes the same
/2.0 immediately after it is read, before any of the downstream arithmetic in
this function runs. Comment updated; it described the old (correct at the time)
each-way basis and would otherwise mislead the next person here.
