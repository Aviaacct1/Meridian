## Method, so this does not circle again

- Take the air service development team's chair first: what makes that buyer choose each
  tier, what makes the director sign, what they will resent. Write it down before pricing.
- Propose AT MOST TWO tier shapes, with a recommendation and the reason, in one screen.
  Every session ends with a numbered decision table John answers yes/no/number; no open
  prose questions. Each answer is logged in W8-STATUS.md with the date and never re-asked.
- Every figure carries its source in the same sentence (John's ruling, a file, or "W8
  proposal"). No figure from training recall. No "illustrative" anywhere client-facing.
- Do not touch code, the deck, the site, the agreement or the manual. W8 writes two files:
  `routes/W8-STATUS.md` and `routes/PRICING-DECISION-2026.md`. Hand John a DevPC git block
  (cd first, commands only) after each session with one commit-message file per commit.
