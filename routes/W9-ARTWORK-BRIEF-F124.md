## 3. Open stand to shell: what the concept must give up

1. **The posts.** Inlay panels sit between aluminium posts. No word, face or line of a chart
   may cross a post. Each panel composes on its own and the wall reads as one across them.
   Overlay may run across posts; confirm with FV before relying on it.
2. **The fascia is Full Vision's.** It carries the exhibiting name, "The Aviation
   Observatory", in whatever form FV allows. It is not part of the wall artwork.
3. **Height and eye line.** The top of each panel is hidden by the fascia at a distance and
   the bottom by the counter and visitors. Put the one-liner in the upper-middle band of the
   back wall, under the spotlights. Exact safe band from FV's panel height.
4. **Photography.** The cleared library images are circa 1536 x 1024 pixels: across a panel
   circa 1m wide that is circa 39 pixels per inch. FV's resolution rule decides, but assume
   they are too small and build the wall from type, colour and vector graphics, as the
   August brief already preferred.

## 6. The checks before John sends

Each file opened and measured at trim and bleed against section 5; every text item inside the
safe margin and clear of posts; colour converted to FV's profile and checked; fonts embedded
or outlined as FV asks; the domain and "The Aviation Observatory" spelt against W6's
confirmation; no figure, no competitor; a one-page proof of each wall for John to read on a
phone. John approves; John sends.

## 7. When to call the paid designer

On 30 September at the latest, if any of these holds: FV asks for native design files
(InDesign or Illustrator) rather than PDF; the colour conversion to FV's profile cannot be
checked; a panel fails a check in section 6 that cannot be fixed the same day. W9's advice:
tell the designer now that a call on 30 Sep or 1 Oct is possible, so the fallback is warm.
