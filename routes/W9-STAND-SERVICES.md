## 1. What the package gives us

One electrical socket, three LED spotlights, one 42-inch floor-standing TV (inputs not yet
known; asked of Full Vision 25 Sep). No internet.

## 2. Wired internet: ordered from Messe Frankfurt, not Informa or Full Vision

Order through the exhibitor manual when the login arrives, or by email. The address Alison
gave displays as telekommunikation@messefrankfurt.com but its link points to a .nl address;
use the manual's online order, or confirm the address before emailing.

**W9's recommendation: 37001177, EUR 1,085.** The demo is a web page served from the Surrey
workstation, so the line carries the screen, not the model; 20/10 gives headroom for the
laptop, the tablet and a pack download at once without paying for the 40 Mbit/s tier. The
WiFi package puts the capture tablet on the same line without our own access point. The 5G
router stays the second route and the Plan B laptop the third (pre-mortem 1). W2 to confirm
the bandwidth is enough before John orders; if W2 says 12/6 suffices, 37000240 at EUR 767.

**Deadline:** not on the price list. TO CONFIRM from the manual, with any late-order charge;
W9 puts the date in the table the day the login arrives.

## 3. Power

One socket for the TV, the demo laptop, the 5G router, the Messe router, the tablet charger
and phone chargers. Carry a German-plug (Schuko) multi-way extension with surge protection,
and UK-to-EU adaptors for anything with a UK plug. Whether extra sockets can be ordered, and
at what price, comes from the manual.

## 4. The screen

The package TV is 42-inch on a floor stand, shown in the back right corner of the default
plan. Full Vision's answer on inputs and USB playback (asked 25 Sep) decides whether the
video loop plays from a USB stick or the demo laptop drives it over HDMI. Carry an HDMI cable
and a USB-C to HDMI adaptor either way.
