## What this list is

Meridian is newly launched. This is the list of things it does not do yet, written down so you
find them here rather than in front of an airline. We update it when it changes and we tell you
when we do.

None of the items below affects the forecast's accuracy record. They are gaps in what the
service produces around the forecast.

## The three we are most often asked about

1. **Frequency has no day-of-week allocation.** A five times weekly route is a weekly count.
   Meridian does not yet say which five days, and a manual Avia forecast would. Where the day
   pattern matters to your pitch, tell us and we will do that part by hand.
2. **Fare is not in the capture score.** The model scores schedule, time of day and access, and
   it does not yet read fare into the share it predicts. Fare shows in the economics, not in the
   capture.
3. **The newest aircraft types are not costable.** 26 types have no published valuation source
   we accept, so their ownership cost, and the route economics that rest on it, are not
   produced. The types that are covered are marked in the output.

## The rest, in the order you are likely to meet them

4. **Optimise can take a few minutes** on a wide search, and the progress display is being
   improved for the launch.
5. **The competition slide is left out of the forecast pack** at the moment. It is coming. It is
   not missing by mistake.
6. **The connecting-traffic floor is under review.** If a connecting number moves between two
   runs of the same route, this is why. It is a setting, not an error, and it will settle.
7. **A city name typed instead of an airport code may not resolve.** Use the three-letter code.
   Where it refuses, it tells you what it could not resolve.
8. **The researched pack quotes fares as bands**, never as a single figure, because of the terms
   on which we licence the underlying data.

## What is not on this list, and why

We do not list the differences between a Meridian pack and a study Avia writes by hand. Those
are not defects; they are the reason both exist. A Meridian pack is the same forecast with
researched support around it, produced the same day rather than in three weeks, so that you can
cover every route on your list. Where a route matters enough for the hand-built study, Avia
still writes it.
