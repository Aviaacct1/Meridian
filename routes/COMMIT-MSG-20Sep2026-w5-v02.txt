THE PRICE CLAUSE, CLAUSE 4.
Launch clients pay 50%, 75% and 85% of the list for years 1, 2 and 3, as fixed cash sums in
pounds, and the published list from year 4. The list is the banded standard price by airport
size, inflated at a flat 3% a year FOR ILLUSTRATION, with the contractual mechanism named as
clause 5. The nine figures, rounded to the nearest £50 as ruled:

  Small  (list £15,000)   £7,500    £11,600   £13,550
  Medium (list £20,000)   £10,000   £15,450   £18,050
  Large  (list £25,000)   £12,500   £19,300   £22,550

The illustrative list is printed to the nearest pound rather than the nearest £50, so that
85% of the year-3 list reconciles with the year-3 cash price. Rounding both would leave a
£50 gap for any buyer who checks the arithmetic in the meeting. W5's judgement call, recorded
in W5-STATUS.md for the controller to overturn.
