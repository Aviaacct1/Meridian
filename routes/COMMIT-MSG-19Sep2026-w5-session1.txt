THE AGREEMENT, DRAFT v0.1, FOR THE SOLICITOR AND NOT FOR A CLIENT.
Fourteen clauses. Fifteen SLOT markers, each carrying the fallback that applies if John
has not ruled the figure by 3 October, and eight LAWYER markers gathered into one list
at the foot so the solicitor can work from that list alone. Nothing is invented: no
price, discount, number of places, expiry, response time or legal wording appears as
settled unless John has ruled it or a source in the repo carries it.

  Clause 4   the published grid, £15,000 / £20,000 / £25,000 a year by airport size,
             from PRICING-HANDOVER-19Sep2026.md. The overage rate is SLOT 9 and has no
             fallback worth the name: the pricing note records that no figure exists
             anywhere and that it must be set before the first licence issues.
  Clause 7   the data position. OAG and Sabre confirmed verbally that outputs may be
             shared; the letters are chased. Flagged to the solicitor as the largest
             exposure in the document.
  Clause 8   the accuracy line verbatim as ruled, and the economics disclaimer exactly
             as it reads in app/aircraft_economics.py DISCLAIMER_FULL. The clause does
             not say the forecast on the client's screen is 89% within 20%, because
             umbrella item 25 has not decided that; if John rules (a), the clause
             stands as drafted.
  Clause 10  data handling as ruled: Avia stores no client data, and stores the
             outputs, the routes run and the named user who ran them. Written so that
             it stays true beside the per-client attribution W2 is building.

THE BUILD, AND WHAT IT VERIFIES.
build_w5_docs.py builds both Word files from their markdown sources and re-opens each
one afterwards: Avia Solutions as author and last-modified-by, en-GB at the document
default and in settings, no run-level or style-level language anywhere else, and no em
or en dash in the file. It fails loudly rather than reporting a build it did not check.
Both files pass. Blocks marked SKIP in the sources are internal notes and never reach
the Word file.
