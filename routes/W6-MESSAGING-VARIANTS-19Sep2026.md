# The four sentences, in The Aviation Observatory's voice

Author: Avia Solutions. Version 3, 20 September 2026. Rewritten to John's rulings of 20
September, 19:30 (W6-RULINGS v3). Version 2's variants are withdrawn: John rejected sentences
1 and 2.3 as written, because they spoke as Avia, and sentence 3 is superseded by
`PRICING-DECISION-2026.md` v1.0.

**The voice.** Every sentence speaks as The Aviation Observatory. Avia Solutions is named only
as the affiliate, and only where it carries the credential. No sentence says "we" meaning Avia,
and no sentence claims Avia's 25 years as the Observatory's own.

## Sentence 1: the one-liner. Two versions, one of them held

The promise is the forecast, not the pack, because the pack is emailed afterwards.

**1-LIVE, cleared for use now.**

> Bring a route to stand F124 and we will build its forecast while you wait, in under five
> minutes.

Longer form, where there is room for the subject: "The Aviation Observatory forecasts any city
pair on earth. Bring one to stand F124 and we will build its forecast while you wait, in under
five minutes."

**1-HELD, drafted and NOT to be used until the controller releases it.**

> Bring a route to stand F124 and we will build its forecast while you wait, in about a minute.

What each claim rests on, from the controller's ruling of 20 September. "Under five minutes"
is claimable now, for Run and Optimise together: Optimise on the narrowed default at 40 to 55
seconds and Run at 8 to 12 seconds warm, evidence `TIMING-20260920-1537`. "About a minute" is
claimable for Run alone, and only once W1 step 2b's restart-proof cold numbers exist, from the
r4 probe this week. Until the controller releases it, no post, page, listing or invitation says
a minute. W6 will not put 1-HELD in front of Jol or John as a choice while it is held, because
the two versions are not interchangeable: one is a claim about the whole demonstration and the
other is a claim about one run.

## Sentence 2: the three sub-messages

**2.1, ruled.** John's words, adopted as they stand.

> The optimal time to fly it, not just how many will fly it.

**2.2, ruled.** Version B with John's rewording of the pack clause.

> You leave with the run you watched, and a full presentation pack follows the same day.

"The same day" holds on every surface until the sender is out of test mode and one pack has
been sent and received over a hotspot at the 11-12 October trial. Nothing says thirty minutes
before then, and the controller releases that wording, not W6.

**2.3, the affiliation clause. Two versions for John.**

> **A.** The Aviation Observatory is affiliated to Avia Solutions, who have forecast routes for
> airports and airlines, independently, since 2001.

> **B.** Meridian is published by The Aviation Observatory, affiliated to Avia Solutions, who
> have built route cases for airports and airlines since 2001 and hold no network position.

A is the controller's draft and the shorter of the two. B carries the independence claim where
it belongs, on Avia, and says what the affiliate actually does, which is the thing a route
development manager recognises. W6 prefers B on the stand and in the invitations, and A where
the space is one line, such as the exhibitor listing.

Both versions attach the credential to Avia and leave the Observatory to speak for the product.
Neither claims independence for the Observatory in its own right, which is what John rejected.
