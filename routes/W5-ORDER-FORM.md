## 5. Launch terms, where this Order Form records them

The Client signs by 30 November 2026 and agrees that it:

1. accepts that Meridian is newly launched and works with TAO on the items on the known-issues
   list attached to this Order Form, rather than treating them as failures;
2. gives TAO structured feedback on use at least once each quarter during year 1;
3. acts as a reference for TAO and allows TAO to use its name and brand under clause 21 of the
   Standard Terms;
4. keeps the licence continuous. If the licence lapses, the remaining launch years are lost, the
   Client returns at the List Price then current, and the re-onboarding fee in clause 11 of the
   Standard Terms applies.

There is no limit on the number of launch clients. The date is the only limit.

## Notes for completion, removed before the form is issued

- The Standard Terms are signed once. This form is signed for each licence and for each change.
- The band follows the count of Covered Airports, never the airport's size. There is no size
  threshold to apply and no passenger number to look up.
- Adding airports later means a new Order Form at the full annual difference, with the renewal
  date unchanged.
- The known-issues list current at signature is attached where the launch terms apply.
- Prices are from `PRICING-DECISION-2026.md` v1.0. If that file changes version, this template
  is rebuilt before the next signature.
