## Why this record exists

The Aviation Observatory Limited licences Meridian to airports and its outputs leave the
building. Both data providers confirmed on calls, with witnesses present, that the service may be
used at World Routes and its outputs shared. The written confirmations are being chased. Until
they arrive, this record is the whole paper trail, so it is written down while the calls are
fresh and filed where the solicitor and any future buyer's diligence can find it.

Complete one block per provider. Where a detail is not remembered exactly, write that it is not
remembered rather than approximating it.

## Block 3: what the record does not cover

Two limits are stated here so that nobody later reads this record as wider than it is.

1. **Airlines are not sold to.** Avia's current Sabre licence does not permit selling to
   airlines. No airline is sold, quoted or priced anywhere, and nothing in these calls changes
   that.
2. **The contracting party has changed.** These calls were held by Avia Solutions Limited. Client
   agreements are signed by The Aviation Observatory Limited. SLOT 6: whether the providers were
   told that, and if not, when they are told, because a buyer's diligence will ask which company
   holds the subscription and which company sells the outputs.
