---

Design the print artwork for a trade-show stand: The Aviation Observatory, launching its
route-forecasting product Meridian at Routes World 2026, Messe Frankfurt, 21-23 October 2026,
stand F124. The audience is airport and airline network planners walking an aisle, not
looking for us. The stand must make them stop.

The brand authority is the attached Identity Standards v1.3. Follow it without exception:
Observatory Ink #0F1B28 as the dominant ground, Paper #F6F3EC, Instrument Grey #C4CDD6, Brass
#D4A249 as the only accent and spent once; Newsreader for statements, Inter tracked for labels
and the web address; no photography; the corner registration ticks are the signature detail.
Always the full name "The Aviation Observatory", "Aviation" at equal weight. The product
lockup is three lines: The Aviation Observatory / Meridian (the emphasis line) / ROUTE
FORECASTING. Never "Meridian by", never product and function on one line. No Avia Solutions
anywhere. No charts, no figures, no accuracy numbers, nothing about any competitor.

The stand (attached sheet): one open side, 4m wide x 3m deep x 2.5m high. Five surfaces,
sizes including 10mm bleed:
- Back wall, the hero, faces the aisle: 3,960 x 2,520mm
- Left wall: 3,015 x 2,520mm
- Right wall: 3,015 x 2,520mm
- Fascia overlay, over the open front: 4,020 x 320mm
- Counter front: 915 x 945mm

What the passer-by can see: a counter circa 945mm tall and a high table with four stools
stand in front of the back wall, and a floor-standing 42-inch TV sits in the back right
corner. Anything below circa 1,700mm on the back wall is hidden. Put everything that must be
read in the band circa 1,750mm to 2,500mm. The plan shows dashed lines at circa 1m intervals
on each wall (possible panel joints, to be confirmed): keep every word clear of them. The side
walls are seen at an angle, so their aisle ends carry their content, high. The back wall must
work alone if the side walls are not printed; never put the only copy of anything on a side
wall.

The words:
- Stopper, the largest type on the stand, top band of the back wall: "Your city pair.
  Forecast in about a minute."
- Under it: "Built from airline schedules and fares, with the method shown." (to be confirmed
  as true; alternative "The optimal time to fly it, not just how many will fly it.")
- Product lockup, same top band, clear of the TV.
- Side walls: the stacked lockup with descriptor, The Aviation Observatory / FORECASTS & THE
  RESIDUAL; the web address (to be confirmed by W6, working value aviationobservatory.com).
- Fascia: The Aviation Observatory, and F124.
- Counter: the primary horizontal lockup (device and wordmark) and the web address.

The register: understated authority, an instrument not a fairground. No stopwatch, timer,
countdown or "60 seconds"; no exclamation marks; "forecast", never "predict".

First, give me five elevations at true proportion, one per surface, with the hidden zone
shaded, for me to review on a phone. Once approved, produce print files: vector PDF, text
outlined, 1:1, 10mm bleed, any raster at 300dpi minimum, CMYK (profile to be confirmed; FOGRA39
as a working assumption), named F124_BackWall_v1.pdf, F124_LeftWall_v1.pdf,
F124_RightWall_v1.pdf, F124_Counter_v1.pdf, F124_Fascia_v1.pdf, author "The Aviation
Observatory".
