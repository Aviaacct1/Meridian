Methodology wording: the forecast is year 1, not "maturity"

Jol's challenge, 19 August 2026: "how can the route be mature in Year 1?" It cannot,
and the sentence was inherited 2025-deck language that does not describe Meridian.
The calibration predicts a route's FIRST year (BT2), and route_forecast applies no
separate ramp because measured maturation on comparable launches (x1.013 to year
two, x1.104 to year three) is the same size as market growth over the period;
applying both would double count. Year 1 is the honest and more conservative claim.
