MOST OF IT WAS THE ADAPTER PASSING NOTHING, not the model failing to produce it.

cortex_app._feed_list already carries code, name, country, base, share, forecast and pdew per
city whenever the engine returns its detail, which is exactly what the contract's connecting
tables want, and the first adapter passed connecting=None and left four blocks empty for no
reason. Now mapped.

DIRECTION IS STATED RATHER THAN ASSUMED. route_feed.feed_side defines beyond as the origin
catchment to the destinations beyond the hub, and behind as the hub's feeders to the route
destination. case["hub_airport"] is the destination, so beyond maps to connecting_at_hub and
behind to connecting_at_destination. Verify on the first deck: the two tables must sum to the
payload's feed_beyond and feed_behind and not to each other's. Getting these the wrong way
round is the error made twice today.

Three more closed. growth_rate now passes the forecast's own projection CAGR, so the deck's
years two and three project on the rate the forecast ran rather than a default nobody chose,
which is GROWTH-IS-THE-GAP applied to the deck. And route_metadata's three fields are filled:
airline_iata from the payload, and the two city codes from the airport code, named as standing
in because the payload carries no IATA metro code and a deck printing the airport where a city
belongs is right far more often than a blank is.

A pdew-only city row is dropped rather than tabled, because a city line with a blank demand
and a blank share is worse than one fewer row.

WHAT REMAINS is segment_forecast.rows, which needs segment_model and the live Sabre cabin
split, and the fare and cost detail in economics_year1. Both are real gaps rather than
plumbing.
