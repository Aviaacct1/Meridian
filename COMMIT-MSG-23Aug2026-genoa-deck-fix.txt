Fix genoa_nyc.py's --ppt path: forecast dict was missing four keys
build_deck() reads directly

Named as a known, non-urgent break in HANDOVER-23Aug2026.md section 8 (out of
scope for the readiness push): genoa_nyc.py --ppt called build_deck() with a
forecast dict shape that predates the current build_deck signature and would
KeyError if run. Fixed now, at John's request, because it is small, contained
to a one-off case script nobody else runs, and carries no risk to the live
served product.

Root cause: route_deck.py's build_deck() forecast-slide stats (line ~107-114)
read forecast['market'], ['captured'], ['feed'] and ['total'] directly, no
.get() fallback. genoa_nyc.py's forecast dict only ever supplied pop, nyc_od,
leaked, repatriated, home_label, subtitle, split, fit_lines, the OLD shape
still named in this module's own docstring, which predates whichever change
moved build_deck onto the new one.

- app/genoa_nyc.py
  forecast dict now also supplies market (the addressable NYC catchment,
  `natural`), captured and total (both `each_way`, the directional demand
  with a nonstop), and feed ("0"). Genoa-NYC has no airline connections
  modelled, it is a repatriation case bounded by GOA's own catchment, not
  the full connecting-feed engine, so feed is genuinely zero here rather
  than an omission.
