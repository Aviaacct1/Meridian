PTEW footing, one level deeper (Jol Kingham, 24 Aug 2026, annotated screenshot)

Jol's follow-up to the two PTEW footing fixes made earlier the same day, this time with
his own working shown: "the PTEW column in the excels don't add - CI says 268 but is 270
sum of the parts, same for the others." His annotated screenshot (Forecast tab and both
Connecting feed legs) showed exactly what he was summing:

- Forecast tab: he summed point to point (120) plus the FOUR competition sub-rows (2, 33,
  36, 76) - not the two leg-total rows above them - and got 267 against a printed GRAND
  TOTAL of 268.
- Connecting feed (SJC behind): he summed the 15 listed city rows plus All-other and got
  35.5 against a printed Total of 35.6. Same pattern on the TPE beyond leg (111.7 v 111.8).

Both are the same rounding-drift issue already fixed once today at the top level (GRAND
TOTAL = sum of the three leg PTEWs), recurring one level further down: the competition
sub-rows and the individual city rows are each independently rounded to a fixed number of
decimal places, so their sum drifts from their own parent total by ordinary rounding error.

Two fixes in app/cortex_workbook.py, both using the same principle already applied to the
GRAND TOTAL row - force the last item in a breakdown to be the remainder against its own
parent, rather than an independently-rounded figure, so the breakdown always foots:

1. Forecast tab competition sub-rows: "O&Ds with direct competition" keeps its own rounded
   PTEW; "O&Ds without direct competition" is now the remainder against the leg row's own
   PTEW (already proven to foot into the grand total). Direct + without now always equals
   the leg total exactly, at both levels of the hierarchy, not just the top.

2. Connecting feed All-other row: its PTEW was computed independently from the residual
   passenger volume (_other / (freq x weeks)). Now computed as the remainder against the
   Total row's own PTEW, less the sum of the displayed city rows' PTEW - the same design
   already used for the row's own demand and forecast columns (both already residuals).
   The full column - every listed city plus All-other - now foots exactly to the Total row
   beneath it, on every leg, on every tab.
