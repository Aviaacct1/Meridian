Competition split: the measured runnable, validated before it is wired

John's ruling, 18 August: the with/without direct competition sub-rows go
back into the client tables, worked out properly, and the replicated CI
case validates the approach against the 2025 analyst's split rates
(0.0/1.5% over TPE, 0.2/4.7% at SJC).

The design honours the k decision rather than re-litigating it: V1 carries
the LEVEL, untouched; the gated QSI feed provides the per-market SHAPE
(two ordinary runs, AVIA_FEED_LEVEL v1 then qsi, read per call); the
script renormalises the shaped market shares to the V1 carried leg totals,
classifies each market against the OAG snapshot week (a beyond market is
competed when any catchment airport flies nonstop to that city; a behind
market when that city flies nonstop to the destination; city codes expand
to same-city airports), and prints bucket capture rates beside the
analyst's reference.

Refusals built in: if the QSI arm returns flat shares (the known
standard-path behaviour), the script says so and stops rather than
shipping a fake split; an empty bucket prints no rate, never a zero.

- app/competition_split.py    the runnable; changes no engine code and no
  shipped number.
- app/test_competition_split.py   11 offline checks on renormalisation,
  the flatness refusal and the bucketing.

The split reaches the workbook and the deck tables only after the CI
validation run reads well; that wiring is the next commit, not this one.
