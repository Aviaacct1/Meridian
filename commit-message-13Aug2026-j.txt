The maturation test, and the half-year union behind a switch that is off

NEITHER JOB IS AN OVERNIGHT ONE and my framing of them was wrong. The six capture files in
bt2_relaxed were written 4 to 5 minutes apart in one sequence on 9 August, 2016 at 17:42
through 2025 at 18:04, so a cohort is circa 5 minutes and only 2016 and 2017 need
rebuilding. BLIND-ON-PIN-COHORTS already timed the refit at 14 seconds. Those mtimes are on
the Dev PC's copy and would be copy times if the folder was robocopied, so timing one cohort
settles it.

bt2/connecting_maturation.py answers GRADING-YEAR-AS-THE-RULER without an arm. backtest
--horizons already grades Y+1, Y+2 and Y+3 but on the P2P leg only, so the connecting
version is two indexed preagg lookups per route with no forecast and no engine. For every
route the arm graded it reads od_p2p and sector_adj at each horizon, reports connecting as
sector less pure P2P, and gives one ratio: connecting at Y2 over Y1 on the same route. It
refuses to run without a preagg store carrying sector_adj rather than full-scanning Sabre
9,000 times, re-checks that sector never reads below pure P2P on the two years RULER-SOUND
never looked at, and counts a route only where both horizons are positive.

THE SAMPLE IS THE LIMIT and it is stated before any number is read. Sabre stops at 2025 and
2020 to 2022 are unusable:

  cohort 2016   Y1 2017, Y2 2018, Y3 2019     all three clean
  cohort 2017   Y1 2018, Y2 2019, Y3 2020     Y3 dropped, COVID
  cohort 2018   Y1 2019, Y2 2020, Y3 2021     Y2 and Y3 dropped, COVID
  cohort 2024   Y1 2025, Y2 2026, Y3 2027     Y2 and Y3 dropped, no Sabre

So the ratio is measurable on 2016 and 2017 only, and the three-point series on 2016 alone.
The 2024 cohort with its 248 reinstatements cannot be spoken for.

THE HALF-YEAR UNION is built in bt2_capture_core.load_legs, which now takes a list of labels
and can read the half-year label alongside the monthly one. The date predicate is untouched:
eff_from and eff_to carry the real dates, so selecting the 15th to the 21st out of a
six-month label is the same question asked of a larger row set and only the label list grows.
_half_year_label returns None for anything that is not a plain YYYY-MM inside 2015 to 2017,
so a single-week label cannot ask for the half-year of a week and 2018 onward cannot be
widened by accident.

IT IS OFF BY DEFAULT AND THAT IS THE POINT. AVIA_BT2_HALFYEAR=1 turns it on. Turning it on
changes capa, qcx and legs_n for every route whose pre-launch month falls in 2015 to 2017,
which is the whole of cohorts 2016 and 2017. It goes on in both chains with a rebuild and a
re-measure, or in neither. That is ONE-IMPLEMENTATION applied to a switch rather than to a
function, and DEFAULT-OFF-SWITCHES is the reason to say so out loud.
