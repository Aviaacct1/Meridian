Meridian beta: known items, please don't report these

A short list of things we already know about, so your first reports are things we don't
know about yet.

1. Some runs, particularly Routes optimisation, take up to a few minutes with no progress
   bar yet. That's expected, not a bug.
2. Frequency shows as a bare weekly count. There's no day-of-week allocation yet, so a
   5x/week route doesn't yet say which five days.
3. The competition slide is deliberately left out of the forecast packs for now. It's
   coming, not missing by mistake.
4. The connecting-traffic floor setting is under active review during this beta and may
   move. If a connecting number looks different between two runs of the same route, that's
   why.

Anything else, particularly anything that looks wrong rather than unfinished, please do
flag it.
