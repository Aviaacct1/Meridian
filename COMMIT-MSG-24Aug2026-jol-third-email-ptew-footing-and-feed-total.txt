Two genuinely separate issues, both in app/cortex_workbook.py, traced before either
was touched:

1. FORECAST TAB FOOTING (267 v 268). Not a basis bug - p2p + behind + beyond equals
   the grand total exactly, by carried_split()'s own invariant (the comment at line
   ~203 already documents this: "the legs sum to the total by construction"). The
   drift was pure independent rounding: each row's PTEW is round(x / (freq x weeks))
   to a whole number, and round(a) + round(b) + round(c) does not generally equal
   round(a+b+c). The GRAND TOTAL row's PTEW column now sums the three displayed PTEW
   values instead of rounding the true total separately, so the row a reader checks
   by hand is the row that is actually right when checked. This is a hard equality
   now (checked in the test as p2p_ptew + sum(leg_ptews) == grand_total_ptew, not a
   tolerance), by construction.

2. CONNECTING FEED TOTAL PTEW - two defects, found together:
   a) The Total row's PTEW cell was blank. A reader summing the visible city rows by
      hand (which is what Jol was doing) was building their own total from
      individually-rounded per-city PTEW figures - a total that drifts from the true
      leg total by ordinary rounding error as the list gets longer, and was never
      going to agree with Cover or Forecast to the unit. Fixed by printing the
      figure directly: the leg's carried total (the same _leg_ew Cover and Forecast
      already use for this leg, both ultimately from the same carried_split(dem)
      call) divided by the route's real freq x weeks - the same formula, same
      source figures as Cover row 24 and the Forecast tabs' own leg rows, so this
      cell agrees with both by construction rather than by chance.
   b) SEVENTH INSTANCE of this week's flat-day PTEW bug, found while fixing (a): the
      "All other connecting markets" row's own PTEW was computed as
      _other / (weeks * 7.0) - a flat, assumed-daily-service denominator, the same
      wrong basis as the six other instances fixed earlier this week (Cover's
      pdew_total, forecast_spec.py's _fc_connecting, etc), just not caught until now
      because this tab had no visible total to check the column against. Fixed to
      freq x weeks, the route's actual scheduled departures. The same wrong
      constant was also present in a fallback at the top of the per-city loop
      (reconstructing "forecast" from "pdew" when a row's own forecast field is
      missing) - fixed for consistency, though it is a rarely-hit fallback path.
