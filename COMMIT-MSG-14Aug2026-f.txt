Contract: scale the city rows on the feed sides, not on feed_total

Follows the previous commit. contract_legs_check caught the fix in it on the
first real run: 15 of 16 contracts passed and the failure was "floor ON as
shipped", the only case with split_floor true.

When the floor is on, route_forecast line 867 has already scaled the feed detail
and both feed sides by conn_carried / feed, and it never reassigns feed, so
demand.feed_total stays the raw pre-floor figure. Scaling the rows by
connecting_carried / feed_total therefore reapplied a scaling that had already
happened, and a top-fifteen subtotal of 49% of its leg became 107% of it.

The denominator is now feed_beyond + feed_behind, which is the feed as the rows
now stand under either setting. The ratio is 1.0 when the engine has done the
work and the cap ratio when it has not, read off the figures rather than off a
flag. Tested on both payload shapes.
