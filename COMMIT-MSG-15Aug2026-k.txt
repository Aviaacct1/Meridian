Competition buckets scaled to their leg: the new invariant caught my own defect

THE CHECK FAILED 16 OF 16 ON ITS FIRST REAL RUN AND IT WAS RIGHT. The competition rows
shipped an hour earlier sit UNDER their leg in the forecast table, and they were not on
the leg's basis. competition_split is built from the feed DETAIL, which is each way and,
with the split floor off, at the pre-cap level; the contract's leg is two-way and
post-cap.

THE MEASUREMENT THAT NAMES IT. On the shipped floor-ON case the hub buckets summed to
15,872 against a leg of 31,745, which is 2.0000x to the passenger. On the floor-OFF
cases the ratio drifts off 2.0 because the detail is also pre-cap: SJC-TPE CI 4x 2028
reads 7,636 against 14,484, SFO-TPE reads 37,552 against 36,920 and GOA-JFK 43,830
against 66,401. One basis difference plus one scaling difference, compounding.

app/forecast_to_contract.py
  Each side is scaled to its own leg by ONE ratio, forecasts to the leg forecast and
  bases to the leg base, so every bucket's SHARE is exactly as the engine measured it
  and only the level moves. This is the fix FLOOR-DOUBLE-SCALED settled for the city
  rows on 14 August, applied to the same shape one table lower.
  The capture rate is RECOMPUTED from the two scaled figures rather than carried, because
  the two scalings differ and the ratio a client reads must be the ratio of the two
  numbers printed beside it. That also corrects the rate: it was each-way forecast over
  two-way market, understated roughly two-fold, and it now sits on the same basis as the
  leg's own capture rate directly above it.
  _competition_scale records both ratios per side, so a reader can see what was applied.

Tested on the measured floor-ON figures: both sides sum exactly to their leg on
forecasts and on bases, and the forecast scale comes out at 2.0001, recovering the
each-way against two-way factor from the data rather than assuming it.

THE INVARIANT STAYS AS WRITTEN. It cost an hour and it earned it: two rows that sum past
the line above them is the fault of 14 August wearing different clothes, and without the
check it would have reached a client table where a reader adds the two rows up.
