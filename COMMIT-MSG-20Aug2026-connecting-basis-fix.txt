Connecting-leg market size doubled to two-way: the basis mix Jol caught live

Jol's finding, reviewing the SJC-TPE packs with John: "connecting market over
Taipei 719,500 both directions... but this says each way" and "connecting over
SJC 185 two-way but this is 185 one-way... there's definitely a mix in there."
Traced to Deck Generator/deck_contract.py's build_contract(): natural, p2p_carried,
cnx_carried and p2p_demand are all explicitly doubled to a two-way basis a few
lines apart in that function, but cnx["hub_market"]/["dest_market"] - which arrive
each way from connecting_from_forecast (dem["feed_beyond_base"]/["feed_behind_base"],
the figure verify_connecting_build.py itself names "each way") - were used
undoubled. One each-way number left in an otherwise two-way contract.

Two measured consequences, both fixed:
- The process-visual chart's "Connecting market over [hub/origin]... both
  directions" boxes printed the each-way figure under a two-way caption.
- The connecting legs' capture rate divided a two-way carried figure by this
  each-way market, reading roughly double the true rate (circa 8.1% shown where
  circa 4.0% is correct on the TPE-beyond leg, verified against the fixture).

Fixed by doubling cnx["hub_market"]/["dest_market"] once, at the top of
build_contract(), and using the doubled figures everywhere the each-way ones
were used before: route_metadata.catchment_headline, summary_and_schedule (the
process-visual chart's own source), and segment_forecast.summary's two
connecting-leg blocks. The destination leg's demand fields were missing
entirely (forecast only) before this fix; added on the same two-way basis as
the hub leg, so the printed table no longer shows a number for one connecting
leg's market size and a dash for the other with no reason behind it.

NOT touched: the Excel workbook (app/cortex_workbook.py) and the self-serve HTML
report (app/pitch_html.py). Both read the raw forecast payload directly, not this
contract, are each-way throughout by deliberate design, and were checked again
today and found correct and self-consistent - this defect is PPTX-only. The BA
LHR-SJC acceptance reference (ba_lhr_sjc_reference(), the hand-curated 2015 deck
numbers) is untouched, checked explicitly in the new test.

- Deck Generator/deck_contract.py: build_contract(), _hub_mkt2/_dest_mkt2 added,
  used in catchment_headline, summary_and_schedule, connecting_at_hub_total,

- Deck Generator/test_connecting_market_basis.py: new, 8 checks, all passing.
