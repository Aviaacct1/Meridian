Add diag_p2p_connecting_split_check.py - does the catchment bug touch the headline split?

John, 21 August: after diag_tpe_sjc_catchment_decomp.py's run 2 showed the Taipei beyond
market is 68x inflated by catchment scope versus a filter effect of 0.95x, reading
route_forecast.py more closely showed the aircraft capacity cap means that inflation
cannot change the route's TOTAL carried passengers (fixed at 139,230 two-way by the
aircraft/frequency). What it CAN do is move how that fixed total splits between P2P and
connecting, via a re-split block whose own comment states it "takes P2P from 62% of the
carried total to 45%" on this exact route - a big, named, already-documented effect.

That re-split takes the MAX of two independent estimates: the engine's own feed-driven
split (which does carry the catchment inflation, via feed_beyond) and split_share's
connectivity-table estimate (p2p_share("SJC","TPE") - static, does not touch the feed
engine at all). Whichever is larger sets the actual split. This script establishes, with
production's own live numbers, which one is actually binding today, and whether fixing
the beyond-side catchment scope would change which one wins.

Two outcomes this can report:
  - if the connectivity floor was already binding, the catchment bug is real but does NOT
    explain the 62%->45% move or affect today's headline P2P/connecting numbers - it would
    only move slide 51's Taipei-vs-San-Jose breakdown inside the connecting total.
  - if the engine's own feed-driven split was binding, the catchment bug DOES explain the
    move, and the 2026 top-line P2P/connecting numbers need correcting before this pitch
    presents, not just slide 51's methodology table.

Built on the same proven pattern as today's two earlier scripts: monkeypatch route_
forecast.forecast() and read its OWN return dict directly (feed_beyond, feed_behind,
connecting_feed, total_demand, carried_forecast, p2p_carried, connecting_carried - key
names confirmed from the source, not guessed), not any wrapping layer. Non-negotiable
sanity check: reconstructing today's connecting_carried from engine_conn/resplit_conn
must match the actual returned figure within 2% before the verdict is trusted.
