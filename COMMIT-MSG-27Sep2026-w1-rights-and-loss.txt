W1: Optimise respects traffic rights on auto-chosen airlines; loss-making picks said (John, TIF-AUH, 26 Sep)

- app/cortex_app.py (_candidate_airlines): an AUTO-CHOSEN airline must have its home country at
  one end of the route, or (EU common aviation area: EU27, NO, IS, CH) be an area carrier on an
  intra-area pair, or already fly the pair in the OAG week. Home country is measured, not listed:
  the country holding most of the carrier's distinct departures in the current OAG week
  (airportsdata). Unknown countries or home: not filtered (flag rather than fill). A NAMED airline
  is never filtered. Found on TIF-AUH, where IndiGo (home IN) was chosen for Saudi Arabia to Abu
  Dhabi. optimised.rights_note names the carriers set aside.
- app/cortex_app.py (api_optimise): optimised.loss_note when the chosen schedule's route margin is
  negative: either it contributes before ownership but loses at the generic ownership cost, or it
  does not cover operating costs and the route is not a proposition on these inputs.
- app/cortex_dashboard.html: both notes on the Optimise basis line.

Known limit, for the record: a carrier whose departures mostly sit outside its registration
country (a pan-European low-cost carrier flying to a non-EU point) can be measured to the wrong
home; the "already flies the pair" rule rescues existing routes, and a named airline is never
filtered.
