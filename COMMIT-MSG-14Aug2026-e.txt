Contract: the legs and the total are now the same route

Four defects in the contract layer, none in the engine, found by reading the
SJC-TPE contract against the 2025 China Airlines deck.

deck_contract wrote the total carried into point_to_point_total. It was written
against assess(), where directional_demand was the local leg. cortex_app sets
demand.total to carried_ew and route_forecast computes that as
min(captured + feed, capacity x plan cap), so the connecting passengers are
already inside it. The contract reported 109,764 as point to point when it is the
whole route, and showed no connecting contribution in the summary.

grand_total then added both connecting legs to a figure that already contained
them, taking 109,764 to 123,266. Against 127,296 seats that is 96.8% with a plan
cap of 87.5%, which is not a forecast because the aircraft cannot carry it.

connecting_at_hub.total was the sum of the city table, and cortex_app._feed_list
takes top=15, so it was the fifteen largest cities presented as a leg: 13,502
against an implied 26,356. forecast_to_contract's docstring says to verify exactly
this on the first deck. The subtotal now travels as top_cities_forecast with the
rule beside it, and the leg total is the carried connecting leg split across the
two sides on the pre-cap feed ratio.

route_forecast scales its feed detail to the carried leg only when split_floor is
on, and every SJC-TPE case runs it off, so the city rows sat at the raw pre-cap
level under a post-cap leg total. connecting_from_forecast now scales the rows to
the carried leg by one ratio, leaving every city's share exactly as measured and
moving only the level, and reports _rows_basis and _rows_scale.

Adds app/contract_legs_check.py: four invariants over a folder of contracts, one
per defect above. Legs sum to the total; the total fits the metal; point to point
is not identical to the total on a route with connecting traffic; a city subtotal
never exceeds its leg. It prints the load factor three ways because their
disagreement is the diagnosis. Tested in both directions on fixtures: the
14 August shape fails, the corrected shape passes.

forecast_to_contract emits p2p_demand_ew, feed_beyond_ew and feed_behind_ew so
the contract can state a demand and a carried figure for the same leg, and can
split the connecting leg without inventing a ratio.

No engine constant and no forecast method changes in this commit.
