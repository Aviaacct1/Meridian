od_source_ab: the DOT switch, A against B on one route

THE RATIO IS MEASURED AND IT IS FLAT. dot_ratio_check on US-to-US pairs touching BOS
and AUS, 2024, 1,516 matched pairs: nonstop 0.959x, connecting 1.098x, total 0.989x.
Cut by the pair's own nonstop share the connecting ratio is 1.091x on pairs with no
nonstop at all, 1.089x under 10%, 1.070x from 10 to 50% and 1.113x at 50% or more.
FLAT ACROSS EVERY CUT, so Sabre's coverage gap is not concentrated in transfer traffic
and the feed can be re-levelled by one number, circa 1.09. The 2.6x to 4.2x pairs in
the earlier SJC/TPA/ORD run were dense-pair noise on top of a flat gap.

WHAT THAT MEANS FOR CAPTURE. Turning the switch on raises US domestic feed markets by
circa 9%, and a fixed capture on a 9% larger market gives a 9% larger feed. The
recommendation is NOT to adjust capture to cancel it: DOT is the census and Sabre the
sample, so the 9% is a correction and cancelling it would be tuning the answer to
preserve the figure we already believe is the less accurate of the two. The test is
whether the US domestic subset of the BT2 back-test improves, which is measurable with
what already exists.

AND IT DOES NOT SPEAK TO JOB 3. SJC-TPE's feed markets are US to Taiwan, which DOT
cannot see at all, so a flat gap on US domestic says nothing about Sabre's
international transfer coverage and the 19% argument is untouched by this.

ONE ANOMALY RECORDED, NOT EXPLAINED. MDW-AUS and AUS-MDW run the other way, 0.52x and
0.54x, consistently in both directions and therefore structural rather than noise.
Both are Southwest strongholds, as is Tampa, which makes it relevant to the TPA-AUS
test case specifically. Untested, and it stays a hypothesis until somebody queries it.

app/od_source_ab.py (new)
  Runs one case through calibrated_forecast twice, control at AVIA_OD_SOURCE=sabre and
  test at auto or dot, and puts the legs side by side: point to point carried,
  connecting carried, beyond, behind, feed total, total carried, natural market and
  captured. Reads the od_source block from each payload, so the run states which source
  answered each leg rather than being assumed to have used the one asked for.
  A route with no all-US market on any leg must not move at all, and the tool says so
  rather than leaving a table of zero changes to be read as a failed run. SJC-TPE is
  the control case for exactly that reason.
