The deck contract is complete: JSON and workbook, from the live forecast

Both write, on all three SJC-TPE cases. 53 of 56 fields carry a figure. Eight blocks at 100%:
catchment, connecting_at_hub, connecting_at_destination, revenue_build, revenue_forecast,
route_metadata, segment_forecast and summary_and_schedule. economics_year1 at 86%.

Yesterday none of them did. run_observatory_pitch's own docstring recorded where we started:
"Nothing in the deck reads a number from it. Do not read the output as a forecast of
anything."

WHAT IS LEFT is three fields and two numbers, and none is a model failure.
avg_ow_fare_connecting is never set by deck_contract at all. cask needs a cost per available
seat kilometre the payload does not carry. ancillary_revenue is a commercial benchmark. And
origin_share at 0.62 and business_share_destination at 0.22 in the segments block are mine
rather than measured, and they scale the whole eight-segment table.

THE CHECK BEFORE ANY OF IT TRAVELS, and it has not been made yet: the average one-way fare in
economics_year1 must land near the econ_fare the economics block already reports. Half or
double means the per-turn reading of economics.revenue is wrong and every figure in that block
is wrong with it. The reading was established from cortex_app building charges_per_turn with
no division, so it is evidenced rather than assumed, but it has not been confirmed against an
output.

TWELVE UNEXERCISED PATHS IN ONE DAY is the finding of the session rather than any single
number. Almost nothing that failed was wrong arithmetic. It was code that had never run on
real data: the mixed-basis script, warm_demo's sign-in, --pairs, the model pickle, five
writer-builder mismatches in emit_workbook, the GeoNames path, and three of my own controls
that reported success without testing anything.

Two rules earned: a control that cannot fail is not a control, and an error report naming the
value but not the place is half a report.
