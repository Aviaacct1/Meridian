the log). Six arms, 2,987 routes paired, no arm short. The connecting-leg
grade is flat (6.0-8.3% within 20%) across a 16.7x range of k and the V1

pre-registered rule fired: V2 does not earn the level. The total-based
table's monotone improvement with k is the compensation pattern predicted
in advance and is not a basis for choosing k.
